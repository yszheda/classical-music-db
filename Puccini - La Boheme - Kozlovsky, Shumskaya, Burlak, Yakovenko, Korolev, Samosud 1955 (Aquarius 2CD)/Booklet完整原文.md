## Page 6 (scans/HIPPO_20241216_0006.jpg) - English translation of synopsis

### Synopsis of La Bohème

**Act I**: In the Latin Quarter of Paris, the poet Rodolfo and his friends Marcello the painter, Colline the philosopher, and Schaunard the musician live a bohemian life in a garret. When the seamstress Mimi knocks on their door looking for a light for her candle, she and Rodolfo fall in love.

**Act II**: They celebrate Christmas in the Latin Quarter. Musetta, Marcello's former lover, appears with the wealthy Alcindoro, and she reconnects with Marcello.

**Act III**: It's winter at the gates of Paris. Mimi is dying of consumption, and Rodolfo and Mimi have separated. Mimi comes to find Rodolfo, and the two former lovers remember their happiness together while Marcello and Musetti quarrel and reconcile.

**Act IV**: Back in the garret, Rodolfo and Marcello are alone. Mimi comes to the garret to die peacefully surrounded by her friends, after saying goodbye she dies, leaving Rodolfo in despair.
