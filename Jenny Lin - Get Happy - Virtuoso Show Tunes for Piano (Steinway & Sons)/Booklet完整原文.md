# Get Happy: Virtuoso Show Tunes for Piano

Jenny Lin presents an album of virtuoso piano arrangements of classic show tunes from the golden age of American musical theatre. These brilliant, entertaining arrangements by some of the twentieth century's greatest pianist-composers bring the glamour and excitement of Broadway into the concert hall, transforming memorable melodies into dazzling display pieces that showcase the piano's brilliance and versatility.

Jenny Lin is one of the most respected young pianists today, admired for her adventurous programming and charismatic stage presence. Her recordings have won her many international awards and widespread acclaim. This album captures her joy in these virtuoso arrangements and her deep love of the American musical theatre tradition.

---

## Page 2

### About the music

The Golden Age of American musical theatre (roughly 1920s-1950s) produced some of the most memorable melodies in all of music, and these songs quickly caught the attention of virtuoso pianists who created brilliant piano arrangements that turned these showstoppers into equally stunning concert pieces. This album brings together many of the best of these arrangements, showing how Broadway melodies could be transformed into virtuoso piano display pieces that retain all their original charm and excitement while adding a new dimension of pianistic brilliance.

The composers represented here are among the greatest songwriters in American history: George Gershwin, Cole Porter, Richard Rodgers, Jerome Kern, Irving Berlin, Harold Arlen, Noël Coward, Leonard Bernstein – each contributed songs that have become part of the very fabric of American culture, songs that continue to be loved and performed all over the world today.

7. **My Funny Valentine** (Richard Rodgers, arranged by Dick Hyman) - 4:01
The beloved ballad from *Babes in Arms*, in a sensitive and beautiful arrangement by Dick Hyman that showcases the melody's haunting quality.

## Page 6 - About Jenny Lin

**Jenny Lin**

Jenny Lin has performed extensively throughout North America, Europe, and Asia, in recital and as soloist with orchestras including the Cleveland Orchestra, the American Composers Orchestra, the Warsaw Philharmonic, and the National Symphony Orchestra of Ukraine, among many others. She has given recitals at Carnegie Hall, the Kennedy Center, Avery Fisher Hall, and the Seoul Arts Center. An active champion of contemporary music, she has given many world premieres and is known for her innovative programming that combines contemporary works with the classic repertoire.

Born in Taiwan and raised in Seattle, Washington, Ms. Lin received her bachelor's and master's degrees from the Juilliard School. She currently lives in New York City. This is her eleventh recording.
