EMMY DESTINN s Oprano
Emmy Destinn, Czech soprano, original name Emilie Pavlina Kittlova, born Prague 26 February 1878, died Ceske Budejovice, 28 January 1930.

Emmy Destinn began her studies in music, learning the violin under F. Lachner and gave her first public concert at the age of eight. She went on to study singing under Marie Loewe Destinn, whose name she adopted for her orofessional career. She made her singing debut in Dresden in 1897, as Santuzza. In 1898 she made her first appearance at the Berlin Opera in Cavalleria Rusticana. International fame came with her performance in the irst Bayreuth edition of Der fliegende Hollander, 1901. She was first heard at he Covent Garden in London in March 1904 (Donna Anna), and returned to sing in London until 1914. On 16th November 1908 she made her debut at he New York Metropolitan in that memorable performance of Aida which also marked the beginning of Toscanini's American career. She returned to New York every year until the 1915/16 season, and created the role of Minnie in La Fanciulla del West, December 1910, singing alongside Caruso under Toscanini's baton. She also sang in Feuersnot (under Richard Strauss, 1901), Butterfly (London, 1905), Erlanger's Tess (1909), Salome (Berlin, 1906; Paris, 1907), Tbe Bartered Bride (1909, under Mahler), Franchetti's Germania (1910), Queen of Spades (1910).
