## Biography

Emmy Destinn, Czech soprano, original name Emilie Pavlina Kittlova, born Prague 26 February 1878, died Ceske Budejovice, 28 January 1930.

Emmy Destinn began her studies in music, learning the violin under F. Lachner and gave her first public concert at the age of eight. She went on to study singing under Marie Loewe-Destinn, whose name she adopted for her professional career. She made her singing debut in Dresden in 1897, as Santuzza. In 1898 she made her first appearance at the Berlin Opera in *Cavalleria Rusticana*. International fame came with her performance in the first Bayreuth edition of *Der fliegende Holländer*, 1901. She was first heard at the Covent Garden in London in March 1904 (Donna Anna), and returned to singing in London until 1914. On 16th November 1908 she made her debut at the New York Metropolitan in that memorable performance of *Aida* which also marked the beginning of Toscanini's American career. She returned to New York every year until the 1915/16 season, and created the role of Minnie in *La Fanciulla del West*, December 1910, singing alongside Caruso under Toscanini's baton.

She also sang in *Feuersnot* (under Richard Strauss, 1901), *Butterfly* (London, 1905), Erlanger's *Tess* (1909), *Salome* (Berlin, 1906; Paris, 1907), *The Bartered Bride* (1909, under Mahler), Franchetti's *Germania* (1910), *Queen of Spades* (1910).
