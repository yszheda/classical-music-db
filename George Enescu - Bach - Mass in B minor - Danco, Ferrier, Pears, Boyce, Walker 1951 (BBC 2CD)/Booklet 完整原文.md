## Memories of George Enescu
### by Yehudi Menuhin

George Enescu had a dream. He wanted to soar through memory, to lose himself in it, to purchase a casket into the realm. Romania where he had spent and it lost time, to relive his own past. I consider it as a personal fact, too long a consideration, his life. Enescu was one of the most gifted musicians, and his music was the most beautiful I had ever heard. His art was very special, with a passion and reverence he taught me well. He had been "born" to the violin because he was always a part of his world. How strange for us!

Enescu was born in 1881 in Liveni-Varnav, a small town in Moldavia. He was a child prodigy, and he studied at the Vienna Conservatory. He was a brilliant violinist and conductor, and he was also a composer of great talent. His compositions include symphonies, chamber music, and operas.

## The Remastering

Renewing a historical sound recording like restoring a painting is a highly delicate process. The recording must be digitized and tonally retouched and transformed to today's audio standards, preserving its original character and the performing artist's intentions without any distortion. Guided by these principles, the recordings in the series "BBC Legends" have been meticulously prepared, using the most modern available technology from Meyer-Jordan Solutions, Spectral Design and others. A wealth of nuance and a dynamic range faithful to the original have been re-created with remastering consisting superior 20- to 24-bit resolution.
