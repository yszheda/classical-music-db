## Arianna led me to a just lament, and Orfeo to a righteous prayer...
*Claudio Monteverdi*

Peri, Caccini and Monteverdi: why was Orpheus the subject of so many of these early operas? The answer almost certainly lies in the fact that the figure unites both words and music. As a poet in ancient Greece, Orpheus sang while accompanying himself on the lyre. His singing was said to charm all living creatures and could cause even the rocks themselves to weep. Orpheus was undoubtedly the best possible ambassador for the new kind of music that was known as recitar cantando.

In this context we generally think of Claudio Monteverdi, who, as we know, was a composer of genius, but his genius had been nurtured by his two predecessors, Jacopo Peri and Giulio Caccini. He knew them well (Orfeo takes its place, therefore, in a logically unfolding relationship, namely, the relation between poets, poets and thinkers to find a lost ideal and to move their audiences by means of an appropriate rhetoric. Reduced to its essentials, music had no other aim but to serve the text.

The programme that is featured in the present album retrace the myth of Orpheus and Eurydice by comparing and contrasting the various interpretations of it by major artists of the time.

### Le nuove musiche

In the years around 1600 music underwent a fundamental change of direction with the birth of opera. It was in Florence that the new genre first emerged as the brainchild of an artistic ideal developed by the Florentine Camerata, a learned society whose members included the city's leading humanists - artists and thinkers. At the time, the Renaissance interest in classical antiquity led them to examine hitherto unpublished sources in order to re-ancient Greek tragedy and poetry, in which they believed that performers had used a voice halfway between speaking and singing, while accompanying themselves on a musical instrument. In order to be able to convey emotions to an audience, music had to comprise not only words but also rhythm and, ultimately, sound.

The art music of the Renaissance was polyphonic in character: whereas the poet declared his passion, it was entirely possible that as many as five singers would declare their love collectively. Moreover, the words were often rendered incomprehensible by the way in which they were sung. Music was compromised by a frequent lack of synchronization between the individual syllables in the different voices and also by improvised ornamentation involving divisions, a technique that musicians tended to apply to excess. As a result, the vocal music of the period raised problems of credibility and intelligibility.

It is easy to understand that what was rediscovery of classical antiquity influenced the new generation of composers, an entirely new type of music coming into being at this time and becoming known as *recitar cantando* or *stile recitativo*. As we shall see in Monteverdi's case, the seconda prattica. The way in which the new style of recitation worked, together with the notation that it employed, was the subject of much theorizing that also found expression in practice. From then on the rhythm and intonation of the singer were intended to imitate the art of recitation as closely as humanly possible.

In order to meet the need for a musical accompaniment to this new vocal thing, instrument makers transformed existing instruments with a view to extending their compass and increasing their range. In many cases that nomenclature evokes the age of classical antiquity: the chitarrone, for example, comprises an echo of the Greek kithara, while the lirone recalls the lyre of Orpheus. This revival was largely improvised by the continuo players, the composer providing only the bass line.

The first operas by Cavalieri, Giulio Caccini and Jacopo Peri, all of whom were members of the Camerata de' Bardi, each wrote a work in the stile rappresentativo based on the model of ancient Greek tragedy.

A another most interesting spectacle is Euridice that was planned for the following year, too. Peri and Caccini were commissioned to write the music and to sing the role of Orpheus and Peri and Caccini agreed between them that the latter would take on the most noble passages that would be sung by his character and Peri and his friends would sing the recitative.

## Il canto d'Orfeo

Peri, Caccini and Monteverdi were merely the first of many composers to devote themselves to Orpheus, a figure who also inspired Domenico Belli, Stefano Landi, Luigi Rossi and Antonio Sartorio, among others. And it was Orpheus who introduced opera to France. Caccini recommended to his friends in the Cardinal du Perron that Rossini wrote his Orfeo for the Fontainebleau court in 1647. Almost half a century after the pioneering exploits of their Italian forebears, Caccini and his librettist Francesco Buti distanced themselves from the antique past of Orpheus and introduced a satirical and comic element to the plot, which is further complicated by the introduction of new deities and supernatural powers, Venus and Cupid.

Antonio Sartorio composed the first Venetian version of the myth, a setting of a libretto by Aurelio Aureli that was first produced in the city in 1672. Opera in Venice was a commercial undertaking dependent for the most part on box-office receipts, and it was in order to meet the expectations of an audience keen to be seduced by extravagant spectacle that Augustinian conventuals, the composer and librettists added secondary characters.

The musical language of Sartorio's opera marks a clear advance on that found in the operas of the earlier period. By then several decades had passed, and the recitatives that were now more expansive, the text of the libretto is less dependent on the vocal line becomes more lyrical. The Florentine recitative becomes more melodic, with shorter phrases from its bass part became increasingly clearly structured and orchestral accompaniments were often doubled over an ostinato. Although the continuo continues to play a predominant role, the melody instruments are no longer restricted to the ritornellos but occasionally accompany the voices.

### La lira d'Orfeo

We have assembled a collection of different instruments that are all replicas of 15th-century models that have come down to us today. The list of instruments itemized in the score of Monteverdi's Orfeo provides us with a basis from which the two violins that enter into a dialogue with a soprano recorder and two cornetts, several violas/violes da gamba are used to add colour to the text and to underscore the affects that are conveyed by the singer. The bowed string instruments, we have two large chitarrones, or theorbo, an arpa doppia, a chitarra spagnola and an organo di legno in the continuo section are attached to a second, extended peg-box in order to increase the compass downwards. These are also three bowed strings from the viol family, a bass viol, a violin/violin da braccio and a lirone (a 12-string viol with a flat bridge that allows the performer to play three- or four-note chords). As for the keyboard instruments, these include an organo di legno, whose principale stop is made of wood, producing a considerably softer sound. Harpsichord with a single 8' stop but a second register of leather plectra and a virginal with gut strings. As far as possible, we have tried to respect contemporary praxis and used un-wound gut strings, short of wind, as well as mean-tone temperament, keyboards with split keys.

Although composers did not normally specify the type of instrumentation that they may have in mind, the continuo instruments play an essential role in the execution of the recitar cantando style. So many parameters leave the interpreter considerable scope for creativity. Monteverdi is one of the few composers to specify in certain - often brief - passages in Orfeo which instruments were used or at what point in the score at the start of the first performance. Since the instrumentation is an integral part of the interpretation, it has had to trust the giudizio - the judgement or discernment - of the performers.

### Il pianto d'Orfeo

We decided that our programme should focus on Orpheus as he appears in key scenes in the first three operas to be devoted to him. Most of Eurydice's entries are taken from the latest operas by Sartorio and Rossi, since these two composers gave her more to sing than their predecessors did. We have also used the music from Rossi's Orfeo (even though the composer had by then lost the bass line), and we used Orpheus's death lament. The key role of the Messenger is symbolized here by a theorbo toccata by Alessandro Piccinini. By way of a prologue we have added Tarquinio Merula's La Cesta con che a comparison of the Orpheus's impossible love for Eurydice and prefigures their tragic destiny.

The operas of Caccini and Euridice some years ago, and it was while we were working together on this project that we met Jacopo Peri and Monteverdi struck us as the obvious next step for us to take. By placing these three composers in their historical context, we have been able to isolate their similarities and their divergence. In sum this has allowed us to experience at first hand the fascinating genesis of a musical language that is capable of expressing the deepest emotions and for us, Orpheus has been the inevitable medium for this new adventure.

*Nicolas Achten, February 2014*

### Le nuove musiche

Dans les années 1600, la musique subit un changement fondamental avec la naissance de l'opéra. C'est à Florence que le nouveau genre émergea pour la première fois, fruit d'un idéal artistique développé par la Camerata florentine, une société savante dont les membres comprenaient les principaux humanistes de la ville - artistes et penseurs. À l'époque, l'intérêt de la Renaissance pour l'antiquité classique les conduisit à examiner des sources jusque-là inédites afin de redécouvrir la tragédie et la poésie grecques antiques, dans lesquelles ils croyaient que les interprètes utilisaient une voix à mi-chemin entre la parole et le chant, tout en s'accompagnant d'un instrument de musique. Afin de pouvoir transmettre des émotions à un public, la musique devait comprendre non seulement des mots mais aussi du rythme et, en fin de compte, du son.

La musique d'art de la Renaissance était de caractère polyphonique : alors que le poète déclarait sa passion, il était tout à fait possible que pas moins de cinq chanteurs déclarent leur amour collectivement. De plus, les mots étaient souvent rendus incompréhensibles par la manière dont ils étaient chantés. La musique était compromise par un manque fréquent de synchronisation entre les syllabes individuelles dans les différentes voix et aussi par l'ornementation improvisée impliquant des divisions, une technique que les musiciens avaient tendance à appliquer à l'excès. En conséquence, la musique vocale de la période soulevait des problèmes de crédibilité et d'intelligibilité.

Il est facile de comprendre que ce qui était redécouverte de l'antiquité classique influença la nouvelle génération de compositeurs, un type entièrement nouveau de musique voyant le jour à cette époque et devenant connu sous le nom de recitar cantando ou stile recitativo. Comme nous le verrons dans le cas de Monteverdi, la seconda prattica. La manière dont le nouveau style de récitation fonctionnait, ainsi que la notation qu'il employait, fit l'objet de nombreuses théorisations qui trouvèrent également leur expression dans la pratique. Dès lors, le rythme et l'intonation du chanteur étaient destinés à imiter l'art de la déclamation aussi fidèlement que possible.

Pour répondre au besoin d'un accompagnement musical pour cette nouvelle chose vocale, les facteurs d'instruments transformèrent les instruments existants dans le but d'étendre leur gamme et d'augmenter leur registre. Dans de nombreux cas, cette nomenclature évoque l'âge de l'antiquité classique : le chitarrone, par exemple, comprend un écho de la kithara grecque, tandis que le lirone rappelle la lyre d'Orphée. Ce renouveau fut largement improvisé par les joueurs de continuo, le compositeur fournissant seulement la ligne de basse.

Les premiers opéras de Cavalieri, Giulio Caccini et Jacopo Peri, tous membres de la Camerata de' Bardi, écrivirent chacun une œuvre dans le stile rappresentativo basé sur le modèle de la tragédie grecque antique.

### Florence, 1600

Ce fut à l'occasion du mariage d'Henri de Navarre et de Marie de Médicis en octobre 1600 qu'Ottavio Rinuccini fut chargé d'écrire les paroles, et Peri et Jacopo Caccini de composer la musique pour les célébrations du mariage. Un autre spectacle des plus intéressants est Euridice qui était prévu pour l'année suivante aussi. Peri et Caccini furent chargés d'écrire la musique et de chanter le rôle d'Orphée et Peri et Caccini convinrent entre eux que ce dernier assumerait les passages les plus nobles qui seraient chantés par son personnage et Peri et ses amis chanteraient le récitatif.

### Le nuove musiche

Negli anni intorno al 1600 la musica subì un cambiamento fondamentale di direzione con la nascita dell'opera. Fu a Firenze che il nuovo genere emerse per la prima volta come il frutto di un ideale artistico sviluppato dalla Camerata Fiorentina, una società dotta i cui membri includevano i principali umanisti della città - artisti e pensatori. All'epoca, l'interesse rinascimentale per l'antichità classica li portò a esaminare fonti fino ad allora inedite per riscoprire la tragedia e la poesia greca antica, nelle quali credevano che gli interpreti avessero usato una voce a metà strada tra il parlare e il cantare, accompagnandosi con uno strumento musicale. Al fine di poter trasmettere emozioni a un pubblico, la musica doveva comprendere non solo parole ma anche ritmo e, in definitiva, suono.

La musica d'arte del Rinascimento era di carattere polifonico: mentre il poeta dichiarava la sua passione, era del tutto possibile che ben cinque cantanti dichiarassero collettivamente il loro amore. Inoltre, le parole erano spesso rese incomprensibili dal modo in cui venivano cantate. La musica era compromessa da una frequente mancanza di sincronizzazione tra le singole sillabe nelle diverse voci e anche dall'ornamentazione improvvisata che coinvolgeva le divisioni, una tecnica che i musicisti tendevano ad applicare in eccesso. Di conseguenza, la musica vocale del periodo sollevava problemi di credibilità e intelligibilità.

È facile capire che quella che era la riscoperta dell'antichità classica influenzò la nuova generazione di compositori, un tipo interamente nuovo di musica che veniva alla luce in questo periodo e divenne noto come recitar cantando o stile recitativo. Come vedremo nel caso di Monteverdi, la seconda prattica. Il modo in cui il nuovo stile di recitazione funzionava, insieme alla notazione che impiegava, fu oggetto di molte teorizzazioni che trovarono espressione anche nella pratica. Da allora in poi il ritmo e l'intonazione del cantante intendevano imitare l'arte della recitazione nel modo più fedele possibile.

Per soddisfare il bisogno di un accompagnamento musicale per questa nuova cosa vocale, i costruttori di strumenti trasformarono gli strumenti esistenti con l'obiettivo di estendere la loro gamma e aumentare la loro portata. In molti casi quella nomenclatura evoca l'età dell'antichità classica: il chitarrone, per esempio, comprende un'eco della kithara greca, mentre il lirone ricorda la lira di Orfeo. Questa rinascita fu in gran parte improvvisata dai suonatori di continuo, il compositore che forniva solo la linea di basso.

I primi operisti di Cavalieri, Giulio Caccini e Jacopo Peri, tutti membri della Camerata de' Bardi, scrissero ciascuno un'opera nello stile rappresentativo basato sul modello della tragedia greca antica.

### Firenze, 1600

Fu in occasione del matrimonio di Enrico di Navarra e Maria de' Medici nell'ottobre 1600 che Ottavio Rinuccini fu incaricato di scrivere le parole, e Peri e Jacopo Caccini di comporre la musica per le celebrazioni del matrimonio. Un altro spettacolo più interessante è Euridice che era previsto per l'anno successivo. Peri e Caccini furono incaricati di scrivere la musica e di cantare il ruolo di Orfeo e Peri e Caccini concordarono tra loro che quest'ultimo avrebbe assunto i passaggi più nobili che sarebbero stati cantati dal suo personaggio e Peri e i suoi amici avrebbero cantato il recitativo.

## Cetra

Replica by Carlos Gonzalez after Gironimo Campi (1600) for the Musée de la musique, Paris

The cetraone (Fr. cittern; Ger. Erzcelter) is a member of the cittern family, instruments with a flat body and metal strings that can be traced back to the medieval period. The word "citterone" is found at a very early date in 17th-century Italian sources and refers to an extended form of cetra in which the longest and most resonant bass strings are attached to an extended peg-box in order to lengthen the instruments' downward range and strengthen the bass register. The use of this specific type of cetra in the 16th and 17th-centuries is symptomatic of our predecessors' fondness for a metallic, metallic sound that Mersenne described as "mazardine" and which also served to enrich the colours of the continuo group.

The Musée de la musique has evolved a philosophy of building replica instruments that meet the demands of its cultural events such as concerts, recordings and performances. This principle privileges the reproduction or reconstruction of certain particularly interesting instruments over the sometimes risky restoration of originals, a process that has often resulted in the distortion and loss of the original instruments' authenticity in ways that are more often than not cannot be reversed.

*Joël Dugot, Curator*
* Musée de la musique, Paris*

## Erzciter

Nachbau von Carlos Gonzalez nach Gironimo Campi (1600) für das Musée de la musique, Paris

Die Erzciter (französisch cittern genannt) zur Familie der Cittern, Instrumente mit flachem Boden und Metallsaiten, die sich in direkter Linie von mittelalterlichen Instrumenten herleiten lassen. Das Wort Citterone erscheint sehr früh in den italienischen Quellen des 17. Jahrhunderts und bezeichnet eine Cetra, bei der die tiefsten und am meisten resonierenden Saiten zur Verlängerung des nach unten gerichteten Tonumfangs und zur Stärkung des Bassregisters an einen erweiterten Wirbelkasten angebracht sind. Die Verwendung dieses speziellen Cetra-Typs im 16. und 17. Jahrhundert ist bezeichnend für die Vorliebe unserer Vorfahren für metallische, grelle Klänge, zusammen mit Mersenne sagte, sie auch dazu dienten, die Farben des Continuos zu bereichern.

Das Musée de la musique hat eine Philosophie des Baus von Instrumenten in Form von Nachbauten entwickelt, deren Reproduktion oder Rekonstruktion bestimmte interessante Instrumente gegenüber den oft riskanten Restaurierungen von Originalen den Vorzug gibt, bei der die Authentizität der Instrumente oftmals in einer Weise verfälscht wurde, die so gut wie nie rückgängig gemacht werden kann.

*Joël Dugot, Konservator*
* Musée de la musique, Paris*
