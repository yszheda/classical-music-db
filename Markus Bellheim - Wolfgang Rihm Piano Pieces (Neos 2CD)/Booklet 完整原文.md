### Inner Page 2 - Introduction

**Wolfgang Rihm (born 1952)**

Wolfgang Rihm is one of the leading German composers of the post-war generation, and his music is characterized by a continuous exploration of musical gesture and expression, combining a modern harmonic language with a deep romantic expression. He has composed in every genre, from opera and orchestral music to chamber music and piano music. His piano music is particularly interesting because it shows his personal approach to the instrument, and his exploration of touch and sonority.

This double CD collection brings together a wide range of his piano music from the 1990s and early 2000s, performed by the young German pianist Markus Bellheim, who has worked closely with the composer and gives deeply committed performances of these challenging works.

---

### Inner Page 3 - About the Performer

**Markus Bellheim**

Markus Bellheim was born in Germany in 1969. He studied piano with Klaus Hellwig and Aloys Kontarsky, and quickly established himself as a leading interpreter of contemporary piano music. He has worked closely with many of the leading composers of our time, including Wolfgang Rihm, and he has given first performances of many important new works. He performs throughout Europe and has recorded several albums of contemporary piano music. He is particularly known for his deep commitment to new music and his ability to bring out the expressive qualities of the most challenging contemporary scores.

---

### Inner Page 4 - Notes on the Music

Rihm's piano music from this period explores the different extremes of the piano's capabilities, from the quietest whispers to the most dramatic outbursts. Each piece explores a single musical idea, developed through Rihm's characteristic continuous variation technique. The titles give a clue to the mood: *Nachruf* (Obituary), *Nacht* (Night), *Gegen* (Against), *Eingang* (Entrance), *Durch* (Through), *Wissenschaft* (Science), *Schwelle* (Threshold), *Nahe* (Near), *Fern* (Far), *Rilke: Tagebuch* (Rilke: Diary), *Valse dehors* (Waltz Outside), *Drei Klavierstücke* (Three Piano Pieces), *Spät* (Late), *Schluss* (Closing).

Each piece is a journey into the sound world of Rihm, where harmony and gesture combine to create a deeply expressive and personal musical experience. Markus Bellheim has been working with Rihm on this project, and his performances have the full authority that comes from close collaboration with the composer.
