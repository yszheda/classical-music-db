# Johann Sebastian Bach
## St Matthew Passion BWV 244

## Edoard van Beinum conducts Concertgebouw Orchestra, Amsterdam Toonkoorst, December 1958

This recording of Bach's St Matthew Passion captures one of the great Dutch performances of the work from the mid-twentieth century, conducted by Edoard van Beinum, one of the greatest conductors to lead the Concertgebouw Orchestra in the post-war period. Van Beinum was celebrated for his deep understanding of the German repertoire, and his Bach playing was always marked by a combination of clarity, structural integrity and deep musicality that made his performances of the Baroque master very special.

This performance from December 1958 brings together a fine cast of soloists, led by the Swiss bass-baritone Heinz Rehfuss as Jesus, and the great Swiss tenor Ernst Haefliger as the Evangelist. Haefliger was one of the most admired Evangelists of his generation, with a clear diction and a lyrical style that perfectly suited this demanding role. Elisabeth Spoorenberg and Maria Hermes are the soprano and alto soloists, with Jan van der Krabben tenor and Tom Loomans bass.

## 第二页 (scans/HIPPO_20221202_0002.jpg)

The St Matthew Passion is one of Bach's supreme masterpieces, composed in 1727 for Good Friday in Leipzig. It sets the story of the Passion and death of Jesus Christ according to the Gospel of St Matthew, interweaving the dramatic narrative with reflective choruses and arias that meditate on the spiritual significance of the events. After Bach's death it fell into neglect before being famously revived by Felix Mendelssohn in 1829, an event that began the great Bach revival that has continued to this day.

This recording was made live at a concert performance in the Concertgebouw Amsterdam in December 1958, and it has been remastered from the original tapes for this release on Audiophile Classics. It preserves a very distinguished performance that shows the best of the Dutch tradition of Bach performance in the mid-twentieth century, before the "authentic performance" movement changed approaches to Bach. This is a big-boned, richly expressive performance that lets Bach's music speak with full weight and great spiritual depth.

## The Artists

**Edoard van Beinum** (1900-1959)

Edoard van Beinum was one of the greatest conductors in the history of the Concertgebouw Orchestra, which he led from 1945 until his sudden death in 1959, only a few months after this performance. He was celebrated for his intelligent and sensitive conducting, with a deep understanding of all schools of composition from Bach to contemporary music. His Bach performances were particularly admired for their clarity and their spiritual depth.

**Heinz Rehfuss** (1917-1988)

Swiss bass-baritone, one of the most respected singers of his generation, he sang all over the world in opera and oratorio, and was particularly admired as Jesus in the St Matthew Passion.

**Ernst Haefliger** (1919-1995)

Swiss tenor, one of the most famous Evangelists in Bach's St Matthew Passion of the post-war period. His clear diction and lyrical voice made him a favourite in this demanding role, and he sang it all over the world with great success.

## 第七页 (scans/HIPPO_20221202_0007.jpg)

**Elisabeth Spoorenberg** (1929- )

Dutch soprano, well-known in the Netherlands in oratorio and opera, she sang regularly with the major Dutch orchestras and choirs.

**Maria Hermes** (1923-2000)

Dutch alto, one of the leading alto singers in the Netherlands in the post-war period, she sang regularly in Bach oratorio performances.

**Concertgebouw Orchestra Amsterdam**

One of the greatest orchestras in the world, with a long tradition of fine Bach performance under many great conductors. In 1958, under van Beinum, it was at the peak of its great tradition.

**Amsterdam Toonkoorst**

The leading Amsterdam amateur choir, long associated with performances of the great choral repertoire in the Netherlands.
