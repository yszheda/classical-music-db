Und immer frischer rauschte

Es singen wohl die Nixen
Tief unten ihren Reih'n.

Und wand're fröhlich nach!
Es geh'n ja Mühlenräder
In jedem klaren Bach.

from its rocky bed,
down to the valley splashing
so blithe and wondrous clear.
I know not what betook me,
who gave me this advice,
I had to follow thither
wayfarer's staff in hand.

Onwards and ever onwards,
still following the stream,
and ever more gaily rippled
and flashed more brightly the stream.

Is this indeed my pathway?
O little stream, speak, which way?
You have with your rippling murmur
confused me wholly in mind.

What say I then of rippling?
This can no rippling be;
The water nymphs truly are chanting
their dances deep below.
Let them chant, my lad, let it ripple,
and light of heart wend your way!
For sure there are mill wheels turning
in every sparkling brook.
