ENGINEERING NOTE: The music of this collection was assembled from a variety of sources. Most of these pieces came from mint condition original records, but in some cases only passable recordings were available to complete the repertoire. Others came from private recordings, of which the favorites received a great deal of wear from frequent listenings by friends and family members of the artists. Successful transfer of the materials to the digital recording medium required significant care and efort. Special styli were chosen to accurately match the individual discs, ensuring maximum frequency response and minimum distortion. The materials were then treated judiciously with modern equipment to remove the surface noise from the 78-RPM pressings. Every effort was taken to avoid removing any musical content during this process. These new masters attempt to maintain the intrinsic qualities of the individual voices.
