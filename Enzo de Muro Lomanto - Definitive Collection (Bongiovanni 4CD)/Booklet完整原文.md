# Enzo de Muro Lomanto (1891-1952)

Enzo de Muro Lomanto was born in Italy in 1891. He made his debut in 1915, and quickly established himself as one of the leading tenors on the Italian operatic scene during the inter-war period. He sang all the leading tenor roles in the Italian repertory at all the major opera houses of Italy, and he was particularly noted for his performances of the Verdi repertory. He continued singing into the 1940s, and died in 1952.

De Muro Lomanto represented the continuation of the great Italian tradition of tenor singing that had been established in the late nineteenth century. He had a clear, bright, ringing tone that was perfectly suited to the heroic tenor repertory, and he was known for his impeccable phrasing and his dramatic intensity. He was one of the last representatives of the old school of Italian tenors before the changes that came after the Second World War.

This collection presents all his surviving recordings in one place for the first time, spanning his entire career from 1925 to 1940. It covers the whole range of his repertory, from the early Verdi through the late Verdi, from Bellini and Donizetti to Puccini and the verismo composers, and includes a selection of Italian art songs and popular songs as well on the fourth disc. It is an invaluable document of the art of one of the last great tenors of the golden age of Italian opera.

# The Golden Age of Italian Tenors

Enzo de Muro Lomanto was one of the leading Italian tenors of the period between the two world wars. He came at the end of a great tradition, the tradition of Italian heroic tenor singing that had been formed in the late nineteenth century. He inherited the clear, ringing tone that characterised this tradition, and he used it with great skill and musical intelligence to sing everything from early Verdi through to verismo.

He sang at all the major opera houses of Italy throughout his career, and he was much admired for the clarity of his diction, the beauty of his phrasing, and the dramatic intensity of his performances. This collection, which brings together all his surviving recordings for the first time, allows us to appreciate again the art of a singer who was much respected in his day, and who deserves to be remembered as one of the finest representatives of his tradition.

Bongiovanni is proud to continue its series "Il Mito dell'Opera", which is dedicated to preserving the memory of the great singers of the past by reissuing their recordings in carefully remastered editions for the modern listener. We hope that this issue will help to secure the reputation of Enzo de Muro Lomanto as one of the great Italian tenors of the twentieth century.
