## Notes

This album contains a live recording of Arrigo Boito's complete opera "Mefistofele" performed at the Lyric Opera of Chicago on October 6, 1965. The recording was produced by IMD Music Distributing S.A. and manufactured in Germany in 2003 under the Living Stage label (catalog number LS 1030).

The performance features an all-star cast including Nicolai Ghiaurov as Mefistofele, Alfredo Kraus as Faust, Renata Tebaldi as Margherita, and Elena Suliotis as Elena, conducted by Nino Sanzogno with the Chicago Lyric Opera Orchestra and Chorus.
