## SYNOPSIS

The action is set in Scotland at the end of the seventeenth century, in the reign of William and Mary, against the background of feuding factions and conflict between Catholics and Protestants. There is a long-standing feud between the Lammermoor Ravenswoods, Lords of Lammermoor, and the Catholic Ravenswoods. The Ravenswoods were defeated and exiled; Lord of Ravenswood had to forfeit his property to the Lammermoors; his father was killed in the conflict.

### Part One: The Parting

**In the woods near Ravenswood Castle**

Lucia with her companions, led by Norman (Normanno), discuss a stranger, seen near the castle. The huntsmen leave hurriedly in search of him. Henry Ashton (Enrico) enters with Raimondo Bidebent (Raimondo), Lammermoor's chaplain. Enrico bitterly deplores his declining fortunes due to political shifts at court, and believes that a politically advantageous marriage between his sister Lucy (Lucia) and a rich local laird can strengthen his position. Raimondo advises caution not to marry Lord Arturo Bucklaw (Arturo). Raimondo diplomatically suggests that Lucia's grief over the recent death of her mother may account for her reluctance. The talkative Norman reveals that, on the contrary, Lucia has been secretly meeting a young man who saved her from a maddened wild bull. The huntsmen return to report that they saw the stranger and recognized him as Edgar of Ravenswood (Edgardo). The furious Enrico swears vengeance on his hated enemy.

**A fountain near Ravenswood Castle**

Lucia, with her maid, her comrades, waits with visible agitation for the arrival of Edgardo. She looks with dread at the ruined fountain and retells the legend of the jealous Ravenswood lover who, according to tradition, killed a girl there. Alisa, seeing her mistress's grief, advises Lucia to forget Edgardo, but Lucia ecstatically believes their love will bring eternal happiness.

**Edgardo comes in hurriedly with news that he must sail at once for France on a diplomatic mission. He proposes first to go to Enrico and endeavor to end the feud between the two families and ask for Lucia's hand in marriage. Fearful that her brother's hatred might part them forever, Lucia insists that Edgardo keep their love a secret. Edgardo once more eternal engagement against Lucia's family, only love, he says, now separates him from his goal. As a symbol of the eternal engagement they exchange rings and give rings as a token of their love.**

### Part Two: The Marriage Contract

#### Act One

**Lord Enrico Ashton's chamber**

Enrico, having intercepted all the letters, has planned his sister's marriage to Arturo. He has intercepted all the letters; Lucia's and Lucia's letters, and a letter has been forged which seems to prove beyond doubt Edgardo's infidelity.

Lucia enters, pale and sad, and rejects scornfully once again as a marriage to Arturo. Enrico produces his forged letter, which Lucia is convinced by, whereupon he will be disgraced and financially ruined unless she consents to marry Arturo. Lucia is nearly numb with grief and devastated by the false letter.

**The cemetery at Ravenswood Castle**

In the pre-dawn chill, among the tombs of his ancestors, Edgardo prepares for the duel, meditating on his lost love and calling on his vengeance. Lucia appears, pale and sad, and rejects scornfully once again as a marriage to Arturo. Enrico produces his forged letter, which Lucia is convinced by, whereupon he will be disgraced and financially ruined unless she consents to marry Arturo. Lucia is nearly numb with grief and devastated by the false letter.

A line of mourners comes from the castle, from which the lamps of the castle are extinguished. The wedding guests are still feasting and dancing. Suddenly Raimondo enters, horror-stricken, and cries out that Lucia has gone mad and killed her husband. Lucia herself appears, pale and bloodstained, her white gown stained with blood, but happy in her madness because she believes herself to be with her lover. In the illusion, she is before the altar where she and Edgardo are to be married. She goes on to promise to pray for him from heaven when he is dead.

Enrico produces his forged letter, which Lucia is convinced by, whereupon he will be disgraced and financially ruined unless she consents to marry Arturo. **The crypt of the Ravenswood family vault**

At the first streaks of dawn, a funeral procession approaches for the burial of Lucia. Edgardo, hearing the tolling of the bell, knows that Lucia is near death. At that very moment, Edgardo, transfixed in grief as the toll bells toll sad message and Raimondo confirms that she is no more. Overcome, Edgardo expresses his hope that he and Lucia, separated on earth, may be reunited in heaven. Before the stunned group of mourners, he draws his dagger and fatally stabs himself. All ask for God's mercy upon him.
