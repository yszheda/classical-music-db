## Liner Notes by Edward Rigler

### EMMANUEL CHABRIER

Emmanuel Chabrier was born in Ambert, Puy-de-Dôme, on January 18, 1841, and died in Paris on September 13, 1894. He was a civil servant for most of his life, working at the Ministry of the Interior while pursuing his musical career. His dual life as bureaucrat and composer gave him a unique perspective on French musical society.

Chabrier's piano music represents some of the most original and colorful contributions to the French piano repertoire. His works show the influence of Wagner, whom he admired greatly (he made a piano reduction of the Tristan und Isolde overture), as well as his love of Spanish music and dance rhythms. The habanera rhythm, in particular, appears frequently in his works.

### PIÈCES PITTORESQUES

The Pièces Pittoresques, composed in 1888, is a collection of ten pieces that showcase Chabrier's mastery of piano color and texture. Each piece paints a different mood or scene:

1. **Paysage (Landscape)** - A serene opening piece that evokes a peaceful countryside view
2. **Mélancolie (Melancholy)** - A deeply felt, introspective piece in C major
3. **Tourbillon (Whirlwind)** - A swirling, virtuosic piece in E minor that lives up to its name
4. **Sous-bois (Under the Woods)** - A mysterious piece suggesting the filtered light and shadows of a forest
5. **Mauresque (Moorish)** - An exotic piece with Middle Eastern flavor
6. **Idylle (Idyll)** - A gentle, pastoral piece
7. **Danse villageoise (Village Dance)** - A lively rustic dance
8. **Improvisation** - A spontaneous-feeling piece that showcases Chabrier's harmonic inventiveness
9. **Menuet pompeux (Pompous Minuet)** - A stately dance with ironic undertones
10. **Scherzo-Valse** - A delightful combination of scherzo and waltz forms

### 5 PIÈCES POUR PIANO

The Five Pieces for Piano, also from 1888, are shorter character pieces:

1. **Aubade** - A morning song, marked "Allegro modéré, molto espressivo"
2. **Ballabile** - A dance movement in F major
3. **Caprice** - Marked "Slow and deliberately," this piece reflects Chabrier's interest in Stravinsky-like freedom
4. **Feuillet d'album** - An album leaf, a brief intimate piece
5. **Ronde champêtre** - A rustic round dance

### BOURRÉE FANTASQUE

The Bourrée Fantasque, composed in 1891, is one of Chabrier's most celebrated piano works. It was first performed by Édouard Risler in 1893. The piece is characterized by its rhythmic vitality and harmonic daring. Chabrier takes the traditional French bourrée dance and transforms it into a virtuosic showpiece that demands considerable technical skill from the performer.

### CAPRICCIO

The Capriccio, completed in 1888, is a substantial work dedicated to Mme. Marguerite Gagne. It shows Chabrier at his most ambitious, with complex harmonies and demanding piano writing.

### IMPROMPTU

The Impromptu dates from 1873 and was dedicated to M. Édouard Marx. Despite its early date, it shows many of Chabrier's characteristic touches: sudden dynamic changes, unexpected harmonic turns, and a sparkling wit.

### HABANERA

The Habanera, composed in 1885, is dedicated to Mlle. Marguerite Lamoureux. The habanera rhythm, which Chabrier encountered during his visits to Spain, became one of his favorite rhythmic devices. This piece is a beautiful example of his Spanish-influenced style.

### AIR DE BALLET

The Air de Ballet dates from 1887 and was dedicated to the pianist Alfred Cortot. It's a graceful piece that showcases Chabrier's gift for melody.

### SUITE DE VALSE

The Suite de Valse, from 1889, is a collection of waltzes that demonstrate Chabrier's mastery of this most French of dance forms. The waltzes range from the tender and melancholic to the brilliantly virtuosic.

### FOUR-HAND WORKS

The album also includes several works for piano four-hands:

**Marche des Cipayes (Sepoys' March)** - From 1865, this early work shows the influence of military band music.

**Trois Valses Romantiques (Three Romantic Waltzes)** - Composed in 1883, these are among Chabrier's most beautiful waltzes, showing the influence of both Schubert and Brahms.

**Cortège Burlesque** - A humorous processional piece that showcases Chabrier's wit and harmonic daring.

**Souvenirs de Munich (Memories of Munich)** - Written between 1868-69, this is a musical souvenir of Chabrier's visit to Munich, where he attended performances of Wagner's operas.

## About the Performer

**Rena Kyriakou** has established herself as one of the leading interpreters of French piano music. Her performances are characterized by their clarity, color, and rhythmic vitality. She has performed throughout Europe, America, and Asia, and has made numerous recordings for various labels.
