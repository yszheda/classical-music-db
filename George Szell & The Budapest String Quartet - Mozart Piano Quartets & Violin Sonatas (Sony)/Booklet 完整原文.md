## George Szell (1897-1970)

Although his family was of Czech origin, George Szell was born in Budapest on June 7, 1897 and spent his childhood and adolescence in Vienna, where he studied music and took his first steps as an interpreter. He was a child prodigy, composing at 6 and making his debut as a piano concerto at the age of ten. A fortuitous incident soon thrust him into the calling in which he would become a master: conductor. He was only 17 when he was asked to replace, on the spur of the moment, the conductor of the Vienna Symphony.

Mozart for piano and violin. The violin is no longer condemned to occasional interjections or incidental imitations: it now alternates with the piano... K.301 has only two movements (a reminder of Johann Christian Bach's two-movement sonatas, as does an Allegro con spirito, and an Allegro, while K.296 has three: an Allegro vivace, an Andante sostenuto and a particularly light Allegro).

## MOZARTS QUARTETTE MIT KLAVIER

Ich halte dieses Werk für hervorragend für diejenigen, die sich mit der Kammermusik beschäftigen. Die beiden Klavierquartette von Mozart gehören zu seinen reifsten Werken und zeigen seine Meisterschaft in der Behandlung der Form und des Ausdrucks.
