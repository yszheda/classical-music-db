## About the Opera

**Der Prozess (The Trial)** is an opera by Austrian composer Gottfried von Einem, based on Franz Kafka's novel of the same name. The libretto was written by Boris Blacher and Heinz von Cramer.

The opera premiered at the Salzburg Festival on August 17, 1953, conducted by Karl Böhm with the Vienna Philharmonic Orchestra. This world premiere recording captures the historic first performance of this important 20th-century opera.

---

## About the Composer

**Gottfried von Einem (1918-1996)** was an Austrian composer, one of the most important Austrian opera composers of the 20th century. His works blend neoclassical and traditional tonal music elements.

---

## About the Conductor

**Karl Böhm (1894-1981)** was an Austrian conductor, one of the greatest Germanic music conductors of the 20th century. He served as principal conductor of the Vienna State Opera and Vienna Philharmonic for many years.
