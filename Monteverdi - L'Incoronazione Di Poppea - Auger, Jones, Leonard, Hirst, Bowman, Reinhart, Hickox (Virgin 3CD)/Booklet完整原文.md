# Claudio Monteverdi
## L'incoronazione di Poppaea
## L'Incoronazione di Poppea

Claudio Monteverdi was the greatest composer of the early seventeenth century Venetian school, and L'incoronazione di Poppea is one of his last and one of his greatest operas, composed in 1642 when he was 75 years old, at the end of his long career. It tells the story of the rise of Poppaea to be the Empress of Rome, the mistress of the Emperor Nero, and the tragic consequences that follow. It is one of the earliest and one of the most powerful operas about the struggle between love and power, and it still grips audiences today with its extraordinary musical depiction of human passion and human ambition.

This recording presents the complete opera performed on period instruments by the English Baroque Soloists conducted by Roger Hickox, with an excellent cast led by the American soprano Arleen Auger as Poppea, the performance captures all the drama and passion of Monteverdi's late masterpiece.

## 第二页 (scans/HIPPO_20221228_0002.jpg)

### The Story

**Act 1**

The opera opens with the goddess Fortune and the goddess Virtue arguing, then Nero the Emperor announces that he will leave his wife Ottavia and marry his mistress Poppea, much to the distress of his former lover and now counselor Seneca, who opposes the marriage. There are various scenes of court intrigue, and the act ends with Poppea successfully advancing her position.

**Act 2**

Seneca is ordered to commit suicide, which he does with great dignity. The news is brought to Nero, who is now secure on the throne with Poppera. Various intrigues continue at court, and various characters plot and counterplot.

**Act 3**

Ottavia is forced to go into exile, Poppaea is crowned Empress of Rome, and the opera ends with the final love duet between Nero and Poppea, celebrating her coronation. The opera is famous for its extraordinary psychological portrait of all the characters, and it explores the nature of power and love in ancient Rome.

## The Artists

**Arleen Auger** (1939-1993) - Poppea

American soprano, one of the most admired singers of her generation, celebrated for her beautiful voice and stylish singing in baroque opera. This was one of her last recordings before her early death.

**Roger Hickox** (1945-1999) - conductor

One of the leading champions of the English period instrument movement, he did much to revive the baroque repertoire and early music revival, and conducted many outstanding recordings of baroque opera for Virgin Classics. He was particularly noted for his dramatic feeling and clear rhythmic vitality in early music.

**English Baroque Soloists

One of the leading period instrument orchestras for Monteverdi and baroque music in the 1990s, with a strong reputation for authentic sounding and expressive performance.
