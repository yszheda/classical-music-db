## Page 2 (scans/HIPPO_20241216_0002.jpg) - Biography of Paul Jacobs

### Paul Jacobs

Paul Jacobs was one of the most admired American pianists of his generation, particularly celebrated for his performances of twentieth-century American and French music. He was born in New York in 1930, and studied with Rosina Lhévinne at the Juilliard School. He later became chairman of the piano department at the Juilliard School, and was also active as a teacher and performer throughout his career. He was particularly noted for his clear-sighted interpretations of contemporary music, and he gave many premiere performances of important works by composers such as John Cage, Aaron Copland, and many others. He was also a great admirer of Debussy's music, and this recording of the complete Etudes stands as one of his great legacies.

## Page 3 (scans/HIPPO_20241216_0003.jpg) - Biography of Gilbert Kalish

### Gilbert Kalish

Gilbert Kalish was born in 1935 in New York. He studied with Adolf Leiberman and Leonard Shure at Tanglewood, and later worked closely with Aaron Copland. He was pianist for many years with the Boston Symphony Chamber Players, and was professor of piano at SUNY Stony Brook, and later at the Curtis Institute of Music. He is particularly known for his championship of American contemporary music, and has collaborated with many leading composers. He joins Paul Jacobs in this recording of Debussy's En blanc et noir for two pianos.

## Page 4 (scans/HIPPO_20241216_0004.jpg) - Liner notes: Debussy's Etudes by Charles Rosen

### Claude Debussy and the Twelve Etudes

by Charles Rosen

When Claude Debussy published his twelve Etudes in 1915, late in his career, they represented the culmination of his lifelong exploration of the piano and of his own unique harmonic and coloristic language. Each etude is not simply a technical exercise but a complete poetic character piece, each one exploring a particular interval, sonority, or technical figuration—what Debussy called "a collection of beautiful pieces for piano." They are among the most profound and beautiful contributions to the piano literature in the early twentieth century.

Paul Jacobs' recording captures the extraordinary variety of colors and textures that Debussy achieved in these marvelous works.

## Page 9 (scans/HIPPO_20241216_0009.jpg) - Notes on En blanc et noir (English)

### En blanc et noir

En blanc et noir was composed in 1915, the same year as the Etudes, for two pianos. It is in three movements, each movement reflecting on different moods and colors. The title suggests the black and white of the piano keys, but also the wartime context of World War I, where Debussy reflected on the situation in music. It is one of Debussy's most perfect late works, combining all of his mastery of color and form.
