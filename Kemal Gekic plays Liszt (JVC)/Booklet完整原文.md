## Work Notes (Japanese original commentary)

### Après une lecture du Dante - Fantasia quasi Sonata

This work was completed in its present form and published in 1849 (Liszt age 40), after many years of refinement. It's from the second volume of *Années de pèerinage* (Years of Pilgrimage), the Italian years, contains 26 pieces, this is one of the most important works. After Dante's *Divine Comedy*, the "Inferno" circle, Liszt depicts the impression in music. It's a difficult piece that requires high technique, rich poetry, colorful sonority, and poetic emotion. Especially famous is the ringing bell passage, the original is masterfully worked.

### Funérailles

This piece was composed in October 1849 (Liszt age 38), title suggests it's a funeral march. It's included in the set of ten *Harmonies poétiques et religieuses*, it depicts the strong reflection of water, from the poetry collection of the same name by Lamartine. It's a threnody for the revolutionaries who died in the 1848 revolution, a heavy, slow piece.

### Les Jeux d'eau à la Villa d'Este

From the third year of *Années de pèlerinage*, this is from the late work of Liszt, it depicts the playing of water at the Villa d'Este fountain, a late impressionistic piece showing the travel impression, free form piano piece from the collection of travel impressions. This is one of the better known pieces from the set, it's a relatively infrequently performed work, the whole piece expresses poetry through music, characteristic use of technique.

### Sonetto di Petrarca No. 104

From the second volume Italian year of *Années de pèlerinage*, based on Petrarch's 14-line sonnet, Liszt set three of Petrarch's sonnets as piano pieces. No. 104 is about passionate love "I hate and I love", the melody expresses this feeling beautifully.

### Fantasy on Two Themes from Mozart's "The Marriage of Figaro"

Arranged by Ferruccio Busoni, edited here by Kemal Gekic. Liszt frequently made piano fantasies from opera themes, this is one of the large scale works, based on two themes from Mozart's *Marriage of Figaro*.

### Mephisto Waltz No. 1

From "Two Episodes from Lenau's Faust", this is one of Liszt most famous works, the depiction of Mephistopheles at the village inn dance, extremely brilliant technique, chromatic tension, rich color, and a sense of urgency.

### La Campanella

From the Paganini Etudes, this is the third etude, famous for its bell-like ringing, a difficult virtuoso piece.

### Liebestraum No. 3

The third of three Liebesträume (Dreams of Love), nocturne-like, short beautiful melody, one of Liszt most popular short pieces.
