## Nikolai Andreyevich Orloff (26 Feb.1892 - 31 Mar.1964), Pianist

Nikolai Orloff (Russian: Николай Орлов), born in Yelets, Oryol Governorate, Imperial Russia, studied Moscow Cons. in 1910 after studying piano with the legendary Konstantin Igumnov and composition and counterpoint with Sergei Taneyev. His first public concert was held in 1912, and he gave the premiere of Alexander Glazunov in the same year. Orloff worked as teacher in Moscow in 1913-1921 and then moved to West, and played in most of the European capitals before coming Britain in 1926. His first engagement was with the Scottish Orchestra in Glasgow and Edinburgh, after which he made his London debut and played continuously with all the leading orchestras and societies in Britain and abroad. A London critic wrote of a 1930 Chopin recital: "Playing such as this deserves extra citation - simply enjoyed every phrase he made his revivified Chopin." He soon took his art to North and South America and he played in practically every country in Europe. In 1948 he settled in Granges-on-Vevey in Scotland and became British subject in 1951. His last recital was in July 1962.

Orloff played repertoire in romantic period (Schubert, Chopin, Tchaikovsky, Franck...) and Russian music. Especially he was one of the most lyrical Chopin player. His first discs being made for Decca 78rpm starting September 1945 when he was 53 years old. Then he made recordings Chopin, Russian composers(represented here), Franck and Ravel for LPs in Italy early 1960s shortly before his death.
