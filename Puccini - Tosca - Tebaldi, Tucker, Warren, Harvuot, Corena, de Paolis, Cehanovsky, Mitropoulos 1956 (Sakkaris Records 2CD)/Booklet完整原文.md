## About this Recording

This is the complete live performance of Puccini's *Tosca* from Rome 7 January 1956, with Renata Tebaldi as Tosca, Richard Tucker as Cavaradossi, Leonard Warren as Scarpia, conducted by Dimitri Mitropoulos with the RAI Rome Orchestra and Chorus. This was one of the great sung performances of the work in the 1950s, captured live in Rome, digitally remastered and issued by Sakkaris Records in 1997, preserving this legendary cast led by Tebaldi and Tucker in one of their greatest roles.

This is the complete original libretto track timings from the performance, organized for reference.
