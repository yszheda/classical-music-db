## Page 4 (scans/HIPPO_20241219_0004.jpg) - English translation of introduction

### Der Rosenkavalier 1946 - The Metropolitan Opera conducted by George Szell

This historic live recording from 1946 captures one of the legendary performances of Richard Strauss' Der Rosenkavalier at the Metropolitan Opera House in New York, with one of the greatest casts ever assembled: Kirsten Flagstad as the Marschallin, Elisabeth Rethberg as Octavian, with Alexander Kipnis as Baron Ochs. George Szell conducts the Metropolitan Opera Orchestra in a performance that has become legendary for its intensity and beauty.

This was one of the great performances after World War II, capturing three of the greatest singers of the era in their prime in one of the most important roles of the German Romantic repertory.
