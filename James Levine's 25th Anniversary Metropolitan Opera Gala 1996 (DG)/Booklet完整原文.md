## Introduction

This recording documents a very special occasion: a gala concert at the Metropolitan Opera, New York, on 27 April 1996, celebrating the twenty-fifth anniversary of James Levine's debut with the company. Levine made his Met debut on 17 June 1971, and has been its Music Director since 1976. This anniversary celebration brought together many of the greatest opera singers of our time for an unforgettable evening of some of the most famous arias and ensembles from the operatic repertoire.

James Levine (born 1943, died 2021) is one of the most important conductors of his generation, and has been particularly associated with the Metropolitan Opera, where he conducted hundreds of performances and overseen the company's artistic growth for decades. This gala concert was a tribute to his extraordinary contribution to the Met and to opera as an art form.

## Participating Artists

Many of the greatest singers of the late 20th century participated in this gala:

- **Tenors:** Plácido Domingo, Alfredo Kraus, Kenneth Riegel, and others
- **Sopranos:** Renée Fleming, Birgit Nilsson, Mirella Freni, Renata Scotto, Kiri Te Kanawa, Karita Mattila, and others
- **Mezzo-Sopranos:** Marilyn Horne, Brigitte Fassbaender, and others
- **Baritones/Basses:** Bryn Terfel, José van Dam, Samuel Ramey, and others

All conducted by **James Levine** with the **Metropolitan Opera Orchestra and Chorus**.

## James Levine at the Metropolitan Opera

- 1971: June 17 – Debut at the Metropolitan Opera
- 1976: Appointed Music Director of the Met
- 1996: 25th Anniversary of debut – Gala celebration
- 2016: Retired after 40 years as Music Director
- Under his leadership, the Met expanded its repertoire, developed its company, and became one of the world's leading opera houses.
