## Page 2 (scans/HIPPO_20241215_0002.jpg) - Biography (English)

### Riccardo Stracciari

Riccardo Stracciari was born in Bologna in 1875, and studied at the Bologna Conservatory. He made his debut in 1896, and quickly became one of the leading Italian baritones of his generation. He sang at all the major opera houses in Italy, and made his debut at La Scala in 1914. He was particularly admired for his Verdi roles, and was one of the greatest Rigoletto interpreters of his time. He continued singing into the 1940s, and taught at the Bologna Conservatory. He died in 1968 in Bologna.

This collection presents his great Verdi baritone arias, recorded between 1909 and 1928, capturing his art at its peak. His voice was described as "a beautiful, warm, bronze baritone, with impeccable diction and perfect legato," and his interpretations are full of dramatic intensity and musical style. This is an important document of one of the greatest Italian baritones of the early twentieth century.
