## Page 3 (scans/HIPPO_20241218_0003.jpg) - Biography of Kate Liu

### Kate Liu

Kate Liu was born in Singapore and moved to the United States with her family when she was thirteen. She studied at the Curtis Institute of Music in Philadelphia with Robert McDonald, where she now continues her graduate studies. She has won many prizes in international competitions, including third prize in the 17th International Chopin Piano Competition in Warsaw in 2015, where this recording was made.

Her playing has been praised for its poetic sensibility, beautiful tone, and deep understanding of Chopin's music. This album contains her prize-winning program from the competition, including the four impromptus, eleven mazurkas, four ballades, the second scherzo, and the heroic polonaise. This is a stunning debut recording from one of the most promising young pianists of her generation.

## Page 5 (scans/HIPPO_20241218_0005.jpg) - Information about the Chopin Competition

### The International Chopin Piano Competition

The International Chopin Piano Competition has been held in Warsaw since 1927. It is the most important and prestigious competition for pianists who specialize in Chopin's music, and it has launched the careers of many of the world's greatest pianists. The 17th competition was held in 2015, and Kate Liu won third prize, moving audiences and critics alike with her deeply poetic and beautifully shaped performances.

This series of CDs from the National Fryderyk Chopin Institute presents the prize-winning performances from the competition, allowing listeners around the world to enjoy these exceptional young artists.
