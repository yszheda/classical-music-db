## VISIONS DE L'AMEN

**AMEN** assumes four different meanings:
- Amen, the Word from Genesis, which is the Apocalypse of the opening. Amen, Word of the Apocalypse which is the genesis of the Consummation.
- Amen, so be it! The Creator's act.
- Amen, I submit, I accept, that Your will be done.
- Amen, the longing, the hope that You will give Yourself to me and to You.
- Amen, with the very fact of their existence, consumed in Paradise.

By joining the life of the beings who say Amen, the varied meanings of Amen are distributed into seven musical visions.

### AMEN OF CREATION

Amen, so be it! *"God said, Let there be light! And there was light!"* (Book of Genesis). The first piano plays a double rhythmic carillon pedal-point in non-retrogradable rhythms. The second piano expresses the theme of Creation, the basic theme of the composition. It begins in absolute pianissimo, in the mystery of this primitive galaxy which already contains the potential power of the Light (and the bells shivering in the light), Light, and consequently Life.

### AMEN OF THE STARS, AND OF THE RINGED PLANET

A brutal and savage dance. Violently spinning stars, saturated with color, Saturn with its multicolored rings. *"God summons them and they are created."*  The second piano exposes the theme of the dance of the planets. First development: polymodal under the turn, first piano varies rhythmically, five notes from the head of the theme. Second development: thematic head with elimination movement opposite right. Third development superimposed: first piano, the beginning of the theme in rhythmic pedal-point; second piano, the theme changes with changes in register. Then repeated the dance of the planets. All these movements together evoke the life of the planets, and the amazing rainbow which colors the spinning rings of Saturn.

### AMEN TO THE AGONY OF JESUS

Jesus suffers and weeps. *"My Father, if this cup may not pass away from me, I accept, may your will be done."* (Gospel according to Saint Matthew). He accepts, may your will be done. The form is that of a Greek triad: Strophe, Antistrophe, Epode. Jesus is alone in the Garden of Olives. There are three musical motifs: 1) God's damnation, 2) a cry in rhythm and that represents the cry that is, the condemnation, 3) an expressive grouping of "upbeat, accent, afterbeat, three fournote rhythmic complaint". Of the three themes that the world gives, varied by rhythmic ostinatos, repeated in different keys, the same gives them to be changed: the very violence of pain becomes a harmony of silence...

### AMEN OF DESIRE

Desire understood in its highest spiritual sense. Thus, the angel named the prophet Daniel: "Man of Desire". There are two themes of desire. The first is slow and ecstatic, the longing of profound tenderness. The second is much more vehement: the soul is drawn by an irresistible love which reaches a crisis of yearning. In the coda the two principal voices seem to melt into one another, and into the harmonious silence of heaven remains...

### AMEN OF THE ANGELS, OF THE SAINTS, AND OF THE BIRDS

Song of the Purity of the Saints: Amen, the exultant singing of the birds; Amen. The angels prostrate themselves before the throne: Amen. First of all the song of the angels and saints, simple and pure. Then, in the midst of the birds' song, an inspiration to a more brilliant piano song. Song of the Saints that becomes all the more pure through nature, and the different noises of nature, a splendid diversity, and the repeated addition of low rhythmic ostinatos, which evoke gong and tam-tams. Episode: repeat of the Saints' song with canons in non-retrogradable rhythms. A brief coda on the bird songs.

### AMEN OF THE JUDGEMENT

Three icy notes, the bells of evidence. Verily, I say unto thee, Amen. *"Depart from me, ye cursed! (Gospel according to Saint Matthew)."* with red, the orange and milk white to green and gold. A full crescendo takes off on the blue-violets and greens, and rises up to the red and gold of the final fanfare of the trumpets, which magnifies the lyric element.

### AMEN OF THE CONSUMMATION

Consummation, Paradise. The life of the glorious body in chimes of light. Then "from transparency to transparency" - The second piano takes up the Theme of Creation. The first piano (combining high and low registers) into it can spin out a long choral glory. The work is condensed rhythmic canons. Sapphire, emerald, topaz, hyacinth, condensed into the rainbow of precious stones of the Apocalypse, dancing, coloring, shocking, resounding, and perfuming the Light of Life.

## LES OFFRANDES OUBLIÉES

*(The forgotten offerings)*

The "Offrandes oubliées" were created on 19 February 191931, at the Théatre des Champs-Elysées. à Paris, under the direction of Walter Straram. I had just turned 22. It was my first work played by an orchestra, and my first contact with the public at large.

The work is in three parts:

**The Cross**: lamentation of the strings, the sorrowful "neumes" of which divide the melody into groups of uneven duration, cut by long mauve and grey wailings. **The Sin**: abyss in an almost "mechanized" speed. You will notice the strong flexional ending accents, whistling of the harmonics in glissando, which rises over a blanket of pianissimo chords, with reds, gold, blues like a frosted stained glass window, in the light of muted solo chords. **The Sin is the forgetting of God. The Cross: "This is my Body, given for you - this is my Blood, spilled for you"*.

## HYMNE

As in my "Préludes" for Piano (written in 1929), this work (which dates from 1932) is above all characterized by its color effects. A first theme, like a gust of wind, uses the colors of the chords. The second theme juxtaposes the colors of three "limited transposition modes". In the development on the first theme, the lyric explosions contrast the transposition limits. These explore the composer's language, with the three modes, gives lilting color combinations on the violin line, the music combines gold and brown to orange striped.
