## Page 3 (scans/HIPPO_20241215_0003.jpg) - Biography in English

### Pavel Lisitsian

Pavel Lisitsian (1911-2004) was one of the greatest Russian baritones of the Soviet period. He studied at the Leningrad Conservatory, and made his debut at the Bolshoi Theatre in 1940. He was a leading soloist of the Bolshoi Theatre for many years, and sang all the major baritone roles in the Russian and Italian repertoire. He was also celebrated as a recitalist, and was particularly admired for his performances of Russian art song, especially the romances of Tchaikovsky. This recording captures him in live concert from 1951 at the height of his powers, performing a program of Tchaikovsky romances and arias, preserved in the Russian State Archive of Phonorecords and released here for the first time.
