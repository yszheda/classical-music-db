# Johann Sebastian Bach
## Selected Keyboard Works
### French Suites • Italian Concerto • Chromatic Fantasy and Fugue

### Diana Boyle fortepiano after Johann Fritz (Vienna c.1740)

This collection brings together some of Bach's most popular keyboard works, all performed on an eighteenth-century fortepiano after the Viennese maker Johann Fritz, whose instruments have a clear and transparent tone that is ideal for Bach's contrapuntal writing. Diana Boyle is one of the leading exponents of Bach performance on period instruments today, and her playing allows every contrapuntal line to emerge with perfect clarity.

### About the French Suites

The six French Suites are among Bach's most popular keyboard works, written probably during the 1720s when he was in Köthen, and they combine dance movements with all the elegance and melodic invention that Bach so effortlessly provides. They are called French not because they are French in style, but because they follow the French order of dance movements that was standard in the eighteenth century. They have been favourite with keyboard players ever since they were published, and they show Bach the keyboard composer at his most entertaining and engaging.

### **About the Artist**

**Diana Boyle** studied with Helmut Walcha, the great twentieth-century Bach interpreter and scholar, and she has specialised in performance on period keyboard instruments for many years. She has devoted her career to the performance of Bach on appropriate period instruments, and has already recorded the complete Art of Fugue and the complete Well-Tempered Clavier on eighteenth-century fortepiano for Divine Art. She is recognised as one of the leading Bach players on historical keyboards before the public today.

### **The Instrument**

The fortepiano used for this recording is a modern copy of an original instrument made by Johann Fritz in Vienna around 1740. The original instrument is now in the Museu de la Música in Barcelona, and the copy was made by Mahomet Kanjanyan, who specialises in copying these early Viennese fortepianos. The instrument has a clear and transparent sound across the entire keyboard, which allows the contrapuntal lines to speak with great clarity, exactly what is needed for Bach's keyboard music.

### **Composer Note**

"Johann Sebastian Bach is the greatest composer of all time for the keyboard, and the French Suites are among his most engaging works. Playing them on this wonderful Fritz fortepiano has been a great pleasure, and I hope that this recording will allow listeners to hear these well-known works in a new light, with a clarity that brings out all the contrapuntal detail that can get lost on a modern piano." — Diana Boyle
