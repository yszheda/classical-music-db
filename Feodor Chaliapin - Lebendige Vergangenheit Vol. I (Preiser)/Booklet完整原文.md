### Feodor Chaliapin

*13 February 1873 – 12 September 1938*

Feodor Ivanovich Chaliapin was the most famous Russian bass of his time, the outstanding singing actor of the century, whose interpretations of such roles as Boris Godunov, Ivan the Terrible, Mephistopheles, Don Basilio and others remain unparalleled to this day. He was born in Kazan on the Volga, and began his career with a travelling opera company at the age of 16. In 1896 he was engaged by the Mamontov Private Opera in Moscow, where he soon became the idol of the public. In 1901 he made his debut at La Scala in Milan, and in 1907 he appeared for the first time in New York and in 1914 in Paris. After the Russian Revolution he left his homeland in 1921 and remained in the West, living in Paris from 1925 until his death in 1938. Even during his lifetime he became a legend, and his art continues to inspire singers to this day.

The present issue, in the series "Lebendige Vergangenheit" (Living Past), presents the first of two volumes of earlier recordings made between 1909 and 1929, which document the art of this extraordinary artist. All recordings have been remastered using the latest technology.
