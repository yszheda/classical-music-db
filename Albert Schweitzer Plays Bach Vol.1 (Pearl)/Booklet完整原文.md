Davidson captures the essence of Schweitzer's Bach performance. Even his choice of registration is rendered unobtrusive, as if to avoid any distraction from the musical elements. The instruments available for his recordings depict a gradual improvement in sound and choice. The first recordings were made in London in 1928 on an organ decided upon by his record company. An article in a Strasburg paper (Neueste Nachrichten, Nov. 11, 1936) mentions "the trials made at that time, Queen's Hall, did not turn out to his satisfaction - the organ was too harsh. He sought in London for an appropriate instrument, and played next on the organ of All-Hallows by the Tower, Barking." The recordings were successful: his entire published session is presented on this volume.
