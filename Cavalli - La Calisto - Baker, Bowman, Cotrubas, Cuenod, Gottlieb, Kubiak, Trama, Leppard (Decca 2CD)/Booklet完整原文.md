## 第一页 (scans/HIPPO_20221120_0001.jpg)

# Francesco Cavalli
## La Calisto

### The English Chamber Orchestra conducted by Raymond Leppard

Francesco Cavalli was one of the leading composers of opera in Venice in the mid-seventeenth century, a pupil of Monteverdi, and he dominated the operatic scene there for decades. *La Calisto*, one of his most charming operas, was first given in Venice in 1651, and it combines a mythological story with a good deal of comedy and some of Cavalli's most beautiful music. It tells the story of Calisto, a nymph who is loved by Jupiter, who disguises himself as Diana to seduce her, with the whole story involving a good deal of comic misunderstanding as well as some genuinely beautiful and moving music.

This recording was made in November 1970, with one of the most distinguished casts that could be assembled at the time, led by the great English mezzo-soprano Janet Baker in the title role, with Iolaina Cotrubas as Diana, Ann Gottlieb as Juno, Hugues Cuenod as Pan, and the English Chamber Orchestra conducted by Raymond Leppard, who did so much to restore the great seventeenth-century Venetian opera to the repertoire. This was one of the classic recordings of the work, and it has been remastered for this reissue on Decca's double CD.

## 第二页 (scans/HIPPO_20221120_0002.jpg)

### The Story of the Opera

**Act 1**

The opera opens in the heavens, with Juno (Juno) suspecting that her husband Jupiter (Jove) is once again chasing after another nymph, Calisto. She is determined to expose him. Jupiter is indeed pursuing Calisto, a beautiful nymph who is a follower of Diana. Jupiter disguises himself as Diana to get close to her, and begins to seduce her. Meanwhile, Endymion is in love with Diana, and Pan is in love with Endymion, adding to the complications. Diana enters with her nymphs, they hunt and bathe, and the scene ends with Jupiter having successfully seduced Calisto in his disguise.

**Act 2**

Juno discovers what has happened and is furious. She curses Calisto, turning her into a bear. Diana finds Calisto and does not recognise her, and hunters are sent after the bear. Endymion and Pan continue their complicated love intrigue. Eventually, the gods intervene on Mount Olympus, and Jupiter placates Juno, and Calisto is placed among the stars as the constellation Ursa Major. The opera ends with a beautiful final chorus.

The opera is a wonderful combination of comedy, sensuality, and beautiful melody, showing Cavalli at his most charming and inventive.

## 第五页 (scans/HIPPO_20221120_0005.jpg)

## The Artists

**Janet Baker** (born 1933)

One of the greatest English mezzo-sopranos of the twentieth century, universally admired for her beautiful voice, intelligent musicianship and moving stage presence. She sang all the great mezzo roles from Baroque to twentieth century, and her Calisto in this recording has remained one of her most beloved performances.

**Raymond Leppard** (1928-2019)

English conductor and scholar, who did more than anyone else in the twentieth century to restore the great neglected operas of the seventeenth-century Venetian school to the stage and the recording studio. His editions of Cavalli and Monteverdi did much to awaken interest in this repertory, and this recording of La Calisto is one of his finest achievements.

**English Chamber Orchestra**

One of the world's most distinguished chamber orchestras, celebrated for their Baroque playing and their versatility in all styles of music.
