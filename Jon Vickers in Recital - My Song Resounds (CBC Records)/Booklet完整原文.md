## Introduction by Kenneth Winters

Jon Vickers ranks among the greatest singers Canada has ever produced. Born in Saskatchewan, raised on a farm, he came to international prominence in the 1950s and enjoyed a magnificent career of more than forty years on the great opera and concert stages of the world. His voice was a powerful, vibrant heldentenor with a unique baritonal color and dramatic intensity that made him unforgettable in heroic roles such as Otello, Peter Grimes, Tristan, and Siegmund.

This recording captures Jon Vickers in recital, live in Edmonton, November 1974. It shows a different side of the great tenor – the art song recital, where the intimacy of communication between singer, pianist and audience creates a different kind of drama, one that depends purely on the word and the music, the expression of deep human feeling through song. Accompanied by Richard Woitach, his long-time recital partner, Vickers is in magnificent voice, giving powerful, deeply felt performances of songs ranging from Baroque to Romantic.

## Page 2 - About the Program

### Alessandro Scarlatti (1660-1725) - Four Songs

Alessandro Scarlatti was the founder of the great Neapolitan school of opera, and a prolific composer of Italian cantatas and songs. These four songs show the expressive power of his vocal writing, with their combination of Baroque formal clarity and deeply emotional expression.

### Henry Purcell (1659-1695) - Four Songs, realized by Benjamin Britten (1913-1976)

Henry Purcell was the greatest English composer of the Baroque period, and his songs are remarkable for their word-setting and expressive power. These four songs were chosen and realized for modern performance by Benjamin Britten, another great English composer who understood and loved Purcell's music. The longest song, "Man is for the Woman made," is a dramatic scena from *The Indian Queen*.

### George Frideric Handel (1685-1759) - Samson: "Total eclipse"

From Handel's great oratorio *Samson*, these recitatives and aria give us Samson after he has been blinded by the Philistines. The aria "Total eclipse" is one of the most powerful arias in all of oratorio, expressing Samson's despair and loss of hope with Handel's incomparable dramatic genius.

## Page 3

### Ludwig van Beethoven (1770-1827) - An die ferne Geliebte (To the Distant Beloved), Op. 98 (1816)

Beethoven's only complete song cycle, *An die ferne Geliebte* is one of the foundations of the Romantic song cycle tradition. The cycle tells of a lover separated from his beloved, finding comfort in nature and in song that he sends across the distance to her. The music is continuously woven throughout the six short songs, with themes that return at the end to bring the cycle to a peaceful and resigned conclusion. It is one of Beethoven's most intimate and heartfelt works.

### Hugo Wolf (1860-1903) - Four Songs from the Italian Songbook

Hugo Wolf's *Italian Songbook* is a collection of 46 songs setting Italian folk poems, remarkable for their condensed expression and perfect word-painting. These four songs show Wolf's genius at its most characteristic: capturing a single moment of emotion in music that is perfectly crafted and unforgettable.

### Franz Schubert (1797-1828) - Three Songs

Schubert wrote more than six hundred songs, and he remains the greatest master of the German lied. Three very different songs are here:
- **Der Atlas** (The Atlas), a powerful dramatic song about a man who carries the weight of the world's sorrows on his shoulders
- **Der Lindenbaum** (The Linden Tree), from the song cycle *Winterreise*, one of Schubert's most beloved melodies
- **Erlkönig** (The Erl-King), the famous dramatic ballad through which Schubert became famous at the age of eighteen.

## Page 4 - Jon Vickers

**Jon Vickers** (1926-2015) was born in Abbotsford, Saskatchewan, Canada, the sixth of eight children on a farm. He studied at the Royal Conservatory of Music in Toronto, and made his operatic debut in 1957. He quickly became one of the most celebrated dramatic tenors of his generation, singing at the Metropolitan Opera, Covent Garden, Bayreuth, and all the great opera houses of the world. He was particularly celebrated for his portrayals of Otello (Verdi), Peter Grimes (Britten), Tristan (Wagner), and Siegmund (Die Walküre).

Throughout his career, he gave many recitals, and he was particularly known for his intense dramatic commitment and deep emotional involvement in everything he sang. He believed that the singer's task was to communicate the deepest human emotions through music, and that commitment is clear throughout this live recital recording from 1974.

## Richard Woitach

**Richard Woitach** (1929-2006) was an American pianist and coach, who worked closely with many of the greatest singers of his generation. He was Jon Vickers' regular recital accompanist for many years, and collaborated with him in recitals throughout the world.
