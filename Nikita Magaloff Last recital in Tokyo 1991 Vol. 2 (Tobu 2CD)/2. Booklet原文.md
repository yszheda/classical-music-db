## 内页文章（英文）
### Nikita Magaloff Piano Recital 14/April/1991
by Sumiaki OHNO

In April 1991, great pianist Nikita Magaloff visited Japan (his last tour in Japan). He had 3 solo recitals and 2 concerto performances. Between his concert tour he also had studio session recordings at that time. After one year (26/December/1992) from this concert tour he died in Vevey, Switzerland. So we can say these performances on this CD are his latest performances.

He visited in Japan for his first time was in 1931! He was 19 years old. At that time he plays as accompanist of Maestro Joseph Szigeti and praised by journalist. It is of curse well known Magaloff married with daughter of Szigeti.

The solo recitals for 2 days in Tokyo 1991 were much expected from audience and journalism. Because since he did not have concert at Tokyo for about 20 years. He showed various programs and many encore pieces by his final noble and elegance style.

14th of April was second day of his recital at Tokyo Metropolitan Theatre. The first piece is Piano Sonata No. 3 by Mozart. It is very rare case we can listen Mozart by Magaloff on CD. He did not leave much recording by Mozart. This piece is not difficult to play by pianist from technical point of view. But therefore it is difficult to make good music by pianist. Recently we can listen many music on internet. Regarding this piece I found much performances by professional pianist or others. For trial I listened some performances by children. I found that all performances are almost same. Again I listened other performances by adult. They are also almost same. Why? I think they just played, in other word just only the piano is ringing. It is difficult to recognize difference from such style of performances. Mozart by Magaloff is totally different from such performances, it is of curse. He showed many freshness in his interpretation. Also he did some detailed tempo changing and nuance changing. We can found them especially from Andante of Mozart.

Also he did not leave much recordings of Scarlatti. But he loved sonata in B Minor, L. 33 and in D Major, L. 361 on this disc. I thought these performance created the sunshine in south Italy by music.

The performance of Sonata in B Minor by Chopin. This is his favorites, dramatic and the sound tone is much brilliant. The accelerando of the final part is remarkable. The performance make listener to imagine brightness of silvery scale of fish in clean river. After Chopin, there was intermission.

The second part of this recital, *Pictures at an exhibition* by Mussorgsky. It is well known Magaloff's origin is Georgia, and he grew up in Russia. The performance of Mussorgsky we can listen roaring of nature animals. Sometimes he show violence expressions. It is very fantastic and exciting, I never heard such performance. Encores for this recital, at first he played his beloved piece *The Lark* by Glinka, it is transcription by Balakirev. This is melancholic beautiful performance. Turkish march by Mozart is very relaxed. At last he played *spinning song* by Mendelssohn. The performance is really noble but not cold.

## 东京艺术剧场介绍（英文）
Tokyo Metropolitan Theatre was opened in October, 1990 by the Tokyo Metropolitan Government as a base to promote art and culture and its globalization among the citizens by presenting quality programs mainly in the fields of music, theatre, opera, and dance. The theatre features a Concert Hall, which is designed exclusively for classical music presentations and boasts one of the world's largest pipe organs, and a Playhouse, Theatre East and Theatre West for plays and dance performances. The theatre is also equipped with four exhibition spaces, large/small meeting rooms, and rehearsal rooms, and is capable of serving multiple purposes in artistic activities by the citizens in addition to actual performances on stage, from art exhibitions to lectures and workshops. Currently, the theatre is operated under the management of the Tokyo Metropolitan Foundation for History and Culture.

A variety of unique music programs have continuously been planned by and presented directly at the theatre, including concerts and lectures that feature our beautiful pipe organ, one of the highlights of our facilities. The theatre also regularly offers classical concert programs with a business partnership with the Yomiuri Nippon Symphony Orchestra, Tokyo. "Theatre Opera" is another quality music program that is popular at the theatre, where the audience enjoys "semi-staged" opera programs.

With its mission to serve as a core facility of new culture, the theatre has also been actively presenting original theatrical productions as well as cosponsored/tie-up programs with outside organizations known for their outstanding artistic performances. In July 2009, the theatre welcomed Hideki Noda, one of the most successful figures in today's theatrical communities in Japan, as its first artistic director so as to pursue further advancement in creative activities and to bring about more quality projects at the theatre. Since then, with supervision of Noda, the theatre has been introducing the audience to a wide variety of programs, from modern versions of classical pieces to experimental works that would not be categorized in any conventional theatrical genres.

On September 1, 2012, the theatre reopened to the public after completing the major renovation that began in spring of 2011. We are ready to serve again the citizens as Tokyo's flagship theatre for music and performing arts. Look forward to our continued progress to create a theatre where art and culture are born; talents and education are supported; people are invited; and intercultural communication is promoted.
