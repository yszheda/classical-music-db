## About the Artists

This "Butterfly" brings together a wealth of American operatic talent.

**Eleanor Steber** (1916-1990) was one of the principal post-war American sopranos. Having initially studied in her native West Virginia she was later taught by Paul Althouse, the distinguished Wagnerian tenor. She made her Metropolitan debut as Sophie in *Der Rosenkavalier* in 1940. This Cio-Cio-San encountered on these discs was the first performance during which she sang the part dramatically, with the exception of several unstaged concert versions. In her biography the Diva confessed to being coached for the role by a Japanese actress in order to lend her portrayal a little more realism. Steber confided, as an aside, that her Kimono for this performance at the Hollywood Bowl had originally been worn by Ginger Rogers for the Film "A Lady In The Dark".

**Richard Bonelli** (1889-1980) the noted American baritone enjoyed a long and successful career. His participation on many opera broadcasts from the 30's and 40's attest to his powerful interpretive abilities. Bonelli studied with Jean De Reszke in Paris before he made his debut at the Brooklyn Academy as Valentin in *Faust* in 1915.

**Jan Peerce** (1904-1984) is probably vividly remembered by most American opera lovers. His vocal longevity allowed him to perform into the 1970's. He made his career primarily as a Radio Artist before he triumphantly debuted opposite Jarmila Novotna and Lawrence Tibbett in *La Traviata* at the Met in 1941.

## About this Recording

This is the complete live performance of Puccini's *Madama Butterfly* from the Hollywood Bowl open-air concert on 3 September 1948, conducted by Eugene Ormandy, with Eleanor Steber as Butterfly, Jan Peerce as Pinkerton, Richard Bonelli as Sharpless. This historic broadcast recording was digitally remastered and issued by Eklipse Records in 1993, preserving one of the great post-war American performances of this beloved opera.
