This "BUTTERFLY" brings together a wealth of American operatic talent. Steber (1916-1990) was one of the principle post war American sopranos. Having initially studied in her native West Virgina she was later taught by Paul Althouse, the distinguished Wagnerian Tenor. She made her Metropolitan debut as Sophie in the Rosenkavalier in 1940. This Cio Cio-San encountered on these discs was the first performance during which she sang the part dramatically, with the exception of several un staged concert versions. In her biography the Diva confessed to being coached for the role by a Japanese actress in order to lend her portrayed a litle more realism. Steber confided, as an aside, that her Kimono for this performance at the Hollywood Bowl had originally been worn by Ginger Rogers for the Film "A Lady In The Dark". Richard Bonelli (1889-1980) the noted American baritone enjoyed a long and successful career. His participation on many opera broadcasts from the 30's and 40's attest to his powerful interpretive abilities. Bonelli studied with Jean De Reszke in Paris before he made his debut at the Brooklyn Academy as Valentin in Faust in 1915. Jan Peerce (1904-1984) is probably vividly remembered by most American opera lovers. His vocal longevity allowed him to perform into the 1970's. He made his career primarily as a Radio Artist before he triumphantly debuted opposite Jarmilla Novotna and Lawrence Tibbett in La Traviataatthe Met in1941. R. K. H
