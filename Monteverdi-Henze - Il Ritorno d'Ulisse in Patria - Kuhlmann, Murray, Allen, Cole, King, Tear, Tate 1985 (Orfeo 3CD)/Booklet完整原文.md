## Introduction

Claudio Monteverdi (1567-1643) composed *Il Ritorno d'Ulisse in Patria* in 1641, to a libretto by Giacomo Badoaro. It was Monteverdi's last opera, composed when he was 74 years old, one of the supreme masterpieces of early Baroque opera.

This is the revised version prepared by the German composer Hans Werner Henze (1926-2012) for performance at the Salzburg Festival 1985, the first performance of this revised edition. Henze provided a freely adapted German performing version, this recording includes complete German text and English translation in the booklet.

The live performance was given on 11 August 1985 at the Salzburg Festival, conducted by Jeffrey Tate, with Thomas Allen as Ulysses, Ann Murray as Penelope, James King as Jupiter, the Vienna Radio Symphony Orchestra, Tölz Boys' Choir. This is the first release of this live recording, digitally remastered 2000 by Orfeo.
