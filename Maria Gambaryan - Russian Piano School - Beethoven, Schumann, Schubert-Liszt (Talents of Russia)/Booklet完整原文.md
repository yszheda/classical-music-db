## Page 4 (scans/HIPPO_20241219_0004.jpg) - English translation of biography

### Maria Gambaryan

Maria Gambaryan was born in Baku in 1955. She graduated from the Moscow Conservatory where she studied with Heinrich Neuhaus and Lev Oborin. After graduation she won the Budapest International Piano Competition and later the Leipzig International Bach Competition. She has performed throughout Russia and abroad in Europe, the United States, Japan, and Latin America. She is currently a professor at the Moscow Conservatory, where she has trained many prize-winning students.

Her playing is rooted in the great Russian piano tradition cultivated by Neuhaus and Oborin. She is particularly admired for her profound interpretations of the German classics, which combine structural clarity with deep poetic feeling and virtuoso technique.

This recording presents her in one of her favorite recital programs, combining Beethoven's dramatic "Tempest" Sonata, Schumann's romantic Kreisleriana, and Liszt's brilliant transcription of Schubert's Erlkönig. This is a representative example of her art from the Russian piano school at its finest.
