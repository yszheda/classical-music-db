### Vladimir Vladimirovich Nielsen (Биография)

Vladimir Vladimirovich Nielsen - more than a pianist, painter, artist, poet - and at the same time thinker, researcher, - he unexpectedly embodied creativity in his art. He was born 1910 in St. Petersburg, spent all his life here. He began studying music in the 1920s. Graduated from the Leningrad Conservatory in the piano class of Heinrich Neuhaus. Already in the third year became a student of organ with Alexander Glazunov. After graduation stayed in Leningrad, taught at the Conservatory.

Vladimir Vladimirovich Nielsen discovered his gift for music more than one talented many-sided personality: he successfully combined piano playing with organ playing, was a painter and poet. It is impossible to imagine his art without these interconnected components of his personality: everything was held together by his natural innate intelligence, impeccable taste and deep musical thinking.

We were same great friends, we were completely like-minded in everything... That's why, after Golubovskaya, the entire artistic nature of Nielsen opened up completely. Opaque blocked my way into music study, and he gave real freedom. From that moment he got out the whole range of his talent.

Nielsen went to organ study with composer Nikolai Golubovskaya already at the conservatory, he immediately got what he needed, Nielsen could realize himself properly. Already in 1936 Nielsen got first prize at the first competition of the Union of Composers. A year later he got second prize at First All-Union Piano Competition.

From the 1930s Nielsen repertoire expanded rapidly, he adopted new works, always ready to perform a new piece at each concert. It turned out that he already knew Bach Mozart Hayden Schumann Schubert like few others, he understood Chopin deeply. His technique is superb, he can play everything from early to modern, from Bach to contemporary composers. His repertoire included over 50 names, he included everything from Bach to Stravinsky Rachmaninov Prokofiev Shostakovich, he constantly kept playing new works, up to the latest compositions.

Nielsen taught at the Leningrad Conservatory for over fifty years. Many famous pianists studied with him. His artistic principle: always come from music to piano playing, not the other way around. His performance amazes with unusual depth, always reveals the composer's thought authentically.

Vladimir Vladimirovich Nielsen was a pianist who embodied the best traits of Leningrad/St. Petersburg culture, and shared its unpredictable outcome after revolution. His origin has Norwegian roots. It's a rare and unique example in history of neighboring countries Russia and Norway.

His grandfather was Johan Samuel Nielsen (1821-1885), Norwegian, came to St. Petersburg from Norway looking for a job around 1850. Being a skilled craftsman, he opened a watchmaking shop on 45 Nevsky Prospect as a second guild merchant, when he got rich bought a tenement house on Ekaterininsky Canal Quay near Voznesensky Bridge in early 1880s.

Of thirteen children of Johan Samuel Nielsen and his wife Therese Winther, only Vladimir Vladimir survived, all others died of tuberculosis. Vladimir Vladimirovich Nielsen was baptized in the Swedish Evangelical Lutheran pastor of St. Catherine's Church, according with Protestant tradition, the child got double name Woldemar- Samuel Vladimirovitch, over time replaced with "Vladimir Vladimirovich", which sounded more familiar to Russian ear.
