# Rudolf Firkušný (1912-1994)

Rudolf Firkušný was a Czech-American pianist, one of the great Czech pianists of the 20th century. He was particularly noted for his interpretations of Czech composers including Janáček, Dvořák, Smetana, and also played Chopin and other Romantic composers with great distinction. This live recital from 1957 captures him at the height of his powers, performing three masterworks of the piano repertoire.

## About the Works

### Chopin: Piano Sonata No. 3 in B minor, Op. 58

Chopin's last piano sonata, composed in 1844, it is one of the greatest piano sonatas of the Romantic period. The powerful opening movement, energetic scherzo, deeply expressive largo, and brilliant finale make it one of Chopin's most monumental piano works.

### Janáček: Piano Sonata "From the Street, 1 October 1905"

Janáček composed this powerful sonata in 1905 after a bloody incident where a Czech worker was killed by Austrian authorities during a demonstration for Czech national rights. The work is full of raw emotion and dramatic intensity, one of the greatest piano sonatas of the 20th century.

### Mussorgsky: Pictures at an Exhibition

Mussorgsky's famous piano work was composed in 1874 as a musical tribute to his friend, the artist Viktor Hartmann. It is a series of musical pictures based on Hartmann's drawings and watercolors, and has become one of the most popular works in the piano repertoire, often performed in the original piano version as heard here.
