## Introduction

Thirty-four-year-old James Levine already in 1977 shaped Mozart's last opera *La clemenza di Tito* with sovereign confidence: this two-act opera seria by Mozart, to a libretto by Pietro Metastasio revised by Caterino Tommaso Mazzola, is presented as a fully valid masterpiece with a brilliant ensemble of singers and an inspiring orchestral sound.

With Werner Hollweg as Tito, Carol Neblett as Vitellia, Catherine Malfitano as Servilia, Tatiana Troyanos as Sesto, Anne Howells as Annio, Kurt Rydl as Publio, the Vienna Philharmonic, and the Chorus of the Vienna State Opera.

In 1977, James Levine, then just 34 years old, delivered an emotionally thrilling interpretation of Mozart's last opera: with aplomb, and as a legitimate master piece featuring a brilliant ensemble of singers and an exhilarating orchestral sound.

## About this Recording

This is the live recording from the 1977 Salzburg Festival, conducted by James Levine at age 34, with the Vienna Philharmonic and an outstanding international cast led by Werner Hollweg as Titus, Carol Neblett as Vitellia, Tatiana Troyanos as Sesto, Catherine Malfitano as Servilia. Orfeo released this historical live performance in 2017 as part of their Salzburg Festival Documents series, preserving this memorable performance from one of the most exciting Mozart conductors of the late 20th century.
