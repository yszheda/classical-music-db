# Déodat de Séverac (1872-1921)

Déodat de Séverac was a French composer, a member of the French impressionist school, closely associated with the region of southern France (Languedoc, Catalonia). His piano music is deeply inspired by the landscapes and folk music of the region, with a very distinctive impressionistic color and atmosphere.

This recording includes three major piano suites by Séverac, performed by the French pianist Jean-Joël Barbier.

## About the Music

Déodat de Séverac's piano music is a perfect example of French impressionism, deeply rooted in the landscape and culture of southern France and northern Catalonia. Each piece paints a musical picture of the region: the arrival by boat, village festivals, pilgrimages, muleteers moving through the mountains, bathing in the sun, a quiet corner of a cemetery in spring. The music is full of warm, colorful impressionistic harmonies and captures the light and atmosphere of the Mediterranean landscape.

---

## Jean-Joël Barbier

Jean-Joël Barbier (1912-1996) was a French pianist, particularly noted for his performances of French impressionist piano music. He studied with Alfred Cortot at the Paris Conservatoire and was a champion of lesser-known French composers including Déodat de Séverac. His recordings are known for their warm, idiomatic interpretations that capture the French impressionist spirit.
