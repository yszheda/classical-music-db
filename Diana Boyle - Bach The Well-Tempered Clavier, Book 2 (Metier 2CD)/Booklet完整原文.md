# Johann Sebastian Bach
## The Well-Tempered Clavier
### Book Two BWV 870-893

Diana Boyle fortepiano after Johann Fritz (Vienna 1742)

The Well-Tempered Clavier is Bach's great keyboard monument, two books each containing twenty-four preludes and fugues, one in each of the twenty-four major and minor keys. The first book was compiled in the 1720s, and the second book was completed later in Bach's life, around 1740, so it represents the culmination of his lifelong exploration of the well-tempered tuning and the possibilities of composition across all the keys.

Like the first book, the second book is a masterpiece that demonstrates the incredible range of Bach's compositional invention across all the keys, each pair of prelude and fugue showing a different aspect of his genius. This recording by Diana Boyle uses a copy of an eighteenth-century fortepiano by the Viennese maker Johann Fritz, giving the music a clarity and a transparency that allows the contrapuntal lines to speak with perfect clarity.

## 第二页 (scans/HIPPO_20211218_0002.jpg)

### About the Artist

**Diana Boyle** studied with the great Bach interpreter Helmut Walcha, and has specialised in performance on period keyboard instruments for many years. She has devoted herself to exploring and recording the complete keyboard works of Bach on appropriate period instruments, and this recording of the second book of the Well-Tempered Clavier completes her project to record all twenty-four preludes and fugues of both books on an eighteenth-century fortepiano. She is recognised as one of the leading Bach scholars and performers on historical keyboards working today, and her previous recording of the first book has been widely praised by critics.

### The Instrument

The fortepiano used here is a modern copy of an original instrument made by Johann Fritz in Vienna in 1742. The original is now in the Museu de la Música in Barcelona, and the copy was made by Mahomet Kanjanyan, who specialises in copying these early Viennese fortepianos. The instrument has a clear and transparent tone that allows the contrapuntal lines to be heard with great clarity, which is ideal for this repertoire.

## 第五页 (scans/HIPPO_20211218_0005.jpg)

[This page contains additional notes on the pieces]

Diana Boyle writes: "The well-tempered tuning allowed Bach to explore all twenty-four keys, and the Fritz fortepiano allows the harmonic explorations to be heard with great clarity. Each prelude and fugue is a separate world, and the instrument's clear registrations help to bring out the individual character of each piece. It has been a privilege to explore this great work over many years on such an instrument, and I hope that this recording will bring the same pleasure to listeners that it has brought me."
