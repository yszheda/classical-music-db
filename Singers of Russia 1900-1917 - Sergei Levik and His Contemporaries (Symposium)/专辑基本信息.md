## 封面引言 (Sergei Levik)

> "On one occasion I was told I was singing wrongly. 'What is right?' I would repeatedly ask anyone who might have the answer. In my search I began to listen to the people around me, how they sang, how they read aloud and even how they spoke. This passion for detailed observation has remained with me all my life and has become, as it were, a second auditory nature."
>
> "有一次，有人告诉我唱错了。'什么是对的？'我会反复问任何可能有答案的人。在我的探索中，我开始倾听周围的人，他们如何歌唱，如何朗读，甚至如何说话。这种对细致观察的热情的伴随我一生，已成为，可以说，第二种听觉天性。"
