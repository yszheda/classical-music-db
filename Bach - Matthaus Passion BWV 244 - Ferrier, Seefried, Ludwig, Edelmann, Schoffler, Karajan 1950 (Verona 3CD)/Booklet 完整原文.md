# Johann Sebastian Bach
## St Matthew Passion (Matthäus-Passion) BWV 244

## Herbert von Karajan conducts Vienna Philharmonic, Vienna Singverein, 1950

The St Matthew Passion is the greatest of Bach's choral works, and one of the supreme masterpieces of all music. It was composed in 1727 for Good Friday, and sets the story of the Passion and death of Jesus Christ according to the Gospel of Matthew, interspersing the dramatic narrative with reflective choruses and arias that meditate on the meaning of the events described.

Bach himself described it as "Passion According to St Matthew", and it was first performed on Good Friday 1727 in the St Thomas Church in Leipzig, where Bach was Cantor. After Bach's death it fell into neglect, like so much of his music, before it was revived by Felix Mendelssohn in 1829, a performance that began the nineteenth-century Bach revival that has continued to this day. Since then it has been one of the most beloved and most frequently performed of all Bach's works.

This recording was made at a single concert performance given in Vienna on 14 December 1950, by the Vienna Philharmonic Orchestra under Herbert von Karajan, and it brings together one of the greatest casts ever assembled for the work. The great Norwegian soprano Kirsten Flagstad and the great English contralto Kathleen Ferrier both took part, together with Irmgard Seefried, Elisabeth Ludwig, Otto Edelmann, Paul Schöffler, Ernst Hafliger and Julius Patzak. It is a extraordinary cast, with some of the greatest singers of the mid-twentieth century, all at the height of their powers.

## 第二页 (scans/HIPPO_20220419_0002.jpg)

Kathleen Ferrier's performance of the alto aria "Erbarme dich" has long been legendary, and this recording preserves that legendary performance for posterity. Her voice was one of the most beloved of her time, with a rich, warm, expressive quality that moved audiences deeply; and here she is caught in her absolute prime, singing the aria that more than any other is associated with her name.

The conducting of Herbert von Karajan is wonderfully concentrated and dramatic, bringing out the intense dramatic power of the score while maintaining the spiritual depth that is at the heart of Bach's great work. The Vienna Philharmonic plays with all the virtuosity and the warm string tone that are its trademark, and the Vienna Singverein Chorus sings with great precision and commitment.

This is one of the most important live recordings of Bach's St Matthew Passion from the mid-twentieth century, and it preserves a performance that any lover of Bach should know. It is issued here for the first time on CD, taken from the original broadcast tapes, and professionally remastered for this release.
