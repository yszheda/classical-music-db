## Rosalyn Tureck Biography

**Rosalyn Tureck**, the eminent pianist and harpsichordist, was born in Chicago on December 14, 1914. There she received her early musical training and piano studies and made her recital and orchestral debut. In 1932 she moved to New York and studied with Olga Samaroff at the Juilliard School of Music, graduating in 1935.

Her career has been almost entirely devoted to the interpretation of the keyboard works of J.S. Bach. Over the course of that career, she made several world tours, primarily as a pianist and harpsichordist, but sometimes as a conductor.

In later years, however, she performed and recorded almost exclusively on the piano. As a teacher, she was on the faculties of several American schools of music, including the Curtis Institute. In 1966 she founded the International Bach Institute and in 1981 the Tureck Bach Institute.

She was one of the most respected Bach interpreters of all time and her playing was admired by many, including the legendary pianist Glenn Gould. Her important book "An Introduction to the Performance of Bach", was translated into several languages.

Rosalyn Tureck passed away in New York on July 17, 2003.
