## 艺术家简介
Ernestine Schumann-Heink (1861-1936) was one of the greatest contraltos in the history of opera. Born Ernestine Rössler in Lieben, near Prague (then part of the Austrian Empire), she made her operatic debut in Dresden in 1878. She went on to establish herself as one of the leading Wagnerian singers of her generation, performing regularly at Bayreuth from 1896 onwards, where she was particularly admired for her interpretations of Erda in the *Ring* cycle and Brangäne in *Tristan und Isolde*.

She made her Metropolitan Opera debut in 1898 and divided her career between Europe and the United States, eventually becoming an American citizen in 1908. Her repertoire was exceptionally wide, ranging from Baroque opera to works by contemporary composers, and she was equally renowned as a recitalist and orchestral soloist.

These 1934 radio broadcasts capture her at the age of 73, still in remarkable vocal form, performing a diverse selection of art songs, traditional melodies, and opera arias, interspersed with personal reminiscences of her extraordinary life and career, including her encounters with Brahms, Wagner, and other leading musical figures of the era. The recordings provide a fascinating glimpse into the artistry and personality of one of the most beloved singers of the late 19th and early 20th centuries.
