### Inner Page 1 - Introduction

**Marian Nowakowski (1898-1969)**

Marian Nowakowski was one of the great Polish basses of the twentieth century, and he had a distinguished career in Poland and internationally. He was born in 1898, studied in Warsaw and in Italy, and made his debut in 1920. He sang at the Warsaw Opera for many years, and also sang all over Europe, including London, Paris, and Italy. He was particularly admired for his intelligent singing, his beautiful deep bass voice, and his dramatic gifts.

This collection brings together recordings made in the 1950s, including many of his greatest operatic arias, plus some song repertoire, showing the range of his repertoire and the quality of his singing. He is accompanied here by the Warsaw Philharmonic Orchestra conducted by Stanisław Skrowaczewski, himself one of the most distinguished Polish conductors of the post-war period.

---

### Inner Page 2 - Biography

Marian Nowakowski was born in 1898 in Warsaw. He studied singing at the Warsaw Conservatory, and then continued his studies in Rome with the great Italian teacher Antonio Cotogni. He made his debut in 1920 in Warsaw, and he became principal bass at the Warsaw Opera in 1923, where he remained until 1958. He sang all the great bass roles, from Mozart and Verdi to Tchaikovsky and contemporary composers. He was also active as a teacher at the Warsaw Conservatory, where he taught many of the next generation of Polish basses. He died in Warsaw in 1969 at the age of 71.

This is the first reissue ever of these recordings, which show what a fine artist he was, with a voice of great beauty and dignity, and a profound dramatic understanding of the music he sang.
