#### Tosca by Giacomo Puccini

#### Victorien Sardou was one of the most popular playwrights in late 19th century France. Several of his dramas were made into operas, including Madame Sans Gene and Fedora by Giordano, and La Tosca by Puccini. The play La Tosca was written in 1887 for the famous actress Sarah Bernhardt, who personally urged Puccini to write music for it. The libretto adaptation he used was one originally prepared for the now forgotten composer Alberto Franchetti, who was talked out of writing it by Puccini's wily publisher, Ricordi. The premiere in Rome, on January 14, 1900, was disrupted by an anti Puccini claque and sniffed at by critics, and indeed critical disapproval has continued to the present day. This has not in the slightest affected the public, who have made Tosca one of the most consistently successful operas in history. It is among a handful of the most frequently performed and enthusiastically attended operas in our time, a fact accounted for by its
