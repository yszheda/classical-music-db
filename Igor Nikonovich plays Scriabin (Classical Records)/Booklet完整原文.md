### Igor Nikonovich

Igor Nikonovich was born in St. Petersburg (Leningrad) in 1958. He studied at the Leningrad Conservatory with Pavel Serebryakov and later at the Moscow Conservatory with Dmitri Bashkirov. He won prizes at the Schubert Competition in Dortmund and the Maria Canals Competition in Barcelona, and has appeared throughout Russia and Europe. He currently teaches at the St. Petersburg Conservatory, and is one of the leading contemporary interpreters of Scriabin's piano music. This recording of the complete piano sonatas by Scriabin is one of the few complete recordings available, presenting all ten sonatas in one authoritative performance.
