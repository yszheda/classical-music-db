## 内页 (Page 20-21) - Synopsis

**Synopsis**

In the palace courtyard of the maids are talking scornfully of Elektra. Despite her constant complaints and abuse, the queen allows her to stay on in the palace. A young maid comes to warn the others, reminding them that the hour for Elektra is being treated like a leper, and that she observes no consideration for the murderers and that according to the view of the murderers and that according to custom she should pay for it all with her blood.

Elektra enters and calls on Agamemnon's spirit — a ritual she enacts daily at the hour of her father's murder. Drunk with ecstasy she imagines the bloody, magnificent feast that she dreams is crowned with royal doom and triumph on the day when vengeance is done.

Chrysothemis with royal doom and triumph wants her against further irritating Clytaemnestra and Aegisthus. She complains that she does not want to wait for her brother's return any longer — five years for two murderers and a woman's lot.

Noises are heard from within the palace. The queen has had another nightmare and wants to see a sacrifice in order to appease the gods. Clytaemnestra leaves in fury but Elektra will confront her mother.

Clytaemnestra emerges from the palace, surrounded with talismans and amulets and followed by her retinue. The train bearer and confidante warn her of Elektra's feigned friendliness, but the queen is more concerned with finding out from her daughter how she can rid herself of her tortuous nightmares, and sends her retinue back into the palace.

Alone with Elektra, the queen describes her dreams and sacrifices which all the magic rites and sacrifices have proved useless. Elektra says that only one sacrifice will lay the ghosts — the death of the murderer. Clytaemnestra refuses to give. The queen does not find answer the riddles and she goes into the palace.

After the queen's exit, Elektra's mood changes to bitterest irony. Elektra conceives a cruel pleasure from describing the purulent wound of her mother by the avenger. Clytaemnestra is paralysed with fear. Elektra's confidante hastens to the palace and whispers something in her ear. The queen sinks down, fainting into the arms of her slaves.

Chrysothemis enters, howling like a wounded animal and explains the cause of Clytaemnestra's collapse: two strangers have just brought word that Orestes is dead. A young servant has been ordered in order to ride off and tell the news to Aegisthus, who is outside the city.

Elektra now tries to persuade Chrysothemis to help her carry out revenge on Aegisthus, and reveals that to this end she has kept and buried the weapon used to murder Agamemnon. She promises her sister that if she personally prepares her wedding bed if she helps her; but Chrysothemis hurries off, horrified at the idea. Elektra decides to carry out the deed by herself.
