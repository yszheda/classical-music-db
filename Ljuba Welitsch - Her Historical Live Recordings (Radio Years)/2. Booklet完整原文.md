## 艺术家介绍
Ljuba Welitsch
Bulgarian-born Austrian soprano (b. Borissovo, 1913)

Ljuba Welitsch (real name Velickova) showed her musical talent at a very early age, initially studying the violin. She then took up singing and studied under T. Lierhammer at the Vienna Academy. She made her debut at the Sofia Opera in spring 1936 and then in Graz, singing in I Pagliacci. She sang regularly in Graz from 1937 to 1940 and then appeared in Hamburg (1941-43), Munich (1943-46) and at the Vienna State Opera (1946-58). In autumn 1947 she was enthusiastically applauded in London, singing in the Vienna Opera's production of Salome, the most famous role in her repertoire and one she had already sung in 1944 in Munich under the direction of Richard Strauss himself. In 1948 she sang in Edinburgh with the Glyndebourne troupe, first as Donna Anna and then as Amelia (Un Ballo in Maschera). Again in 1948 she made her debut at the Metropolitan in New York. Considered one of the most fascinating singers of the post-war years she boasted remarkable stage presence and total identification with the characters of her favourite roles, first of which was Salome, but also Amelia, Tosca, Aida, Musetta, Jenufa, Minnie (Fanciulla del West), Nadja (Salmhofer's Ivan Tarassenko), Rosalinda (Fledermaus), Lisa (The Queen of Hearts), Ilona (Zigeunerliebe) and Hanna (Merry Widow). She was an outstanding prima donna in both opera and operetta with a voice of striking drama and pathos.
