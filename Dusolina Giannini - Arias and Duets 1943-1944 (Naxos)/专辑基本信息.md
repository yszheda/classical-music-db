# 专辑基本信息

## 专辑信息
- **专辑名**: In Concert: Dusolina Giannini - Arias and Duets
- **艺术家**: Dusolina Giannini (杜索琳娜·詹尼尼)
- **内容**: 咏叹调与二重唱，选自：
  - Cavalleria rusticana (乡村骑士)
  - La Forza del destino (命运之力)
  - La Gioconda (歌女乔孔达)
  - Mignon (迷娘)
  - Tosca (托斯卡)
  - Aida (阿依达)
- **录音时间**: Broadcast excerpts: 15th August and 19th September 1943 and 30th January, 1944

- San Francisco Symphony Orchestra
- Los Angeles Philharmonic Orchestra
- Conductors: Pietro Cimini, Gaetano Merola
- 特邀: Claramae Turner, contralto (在《乡村骑士》中)
