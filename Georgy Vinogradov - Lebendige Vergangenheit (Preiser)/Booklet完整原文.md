### Georgy Vinogradov

*1899 – 1980*

Georgy Vinogradov was one of the most famous Russian tenors of the Soviet period. He was born in 1899 in Shturmanskoye (now in Sverdlovsk region) and studied at the Moscow Conservatory with Leonid Sobinov, the great Russian tenor. He began his career at the Sverdlovsk Opera in 1923, and from 1927 until 1958 he was a leading tenor at the Bolshoi Theatre in Moscow. He was one of the most popular singers of his time in the Soviet Union, admired for the beautiful quality of his voice and his sensitive musicianship. He was particularly famous for his performances of lyric roles and for Russian art songs. He gave many concerts throughout the Soviet Union, and his recordings were extremely popular. After retiring from the Bolshoi Theatre he taught at the Moscow Conservatory. He died in Moscow in 1980.

The present issue in the series "Lebendige Vergangenheit" (Living Past) presents a selection of his recordings made between 1933 and 1951, which have been remastered using the latest technology.
