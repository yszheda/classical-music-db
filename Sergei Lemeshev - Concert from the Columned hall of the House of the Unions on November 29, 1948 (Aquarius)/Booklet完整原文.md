## Page 3 (scans/HIPPO_20241217_0003.jpg) - Biography English translation

### Sergei Lemeshev

Sergei Lemeshev (1902-1977) was one of the greatest Russian tenors of the Soviet period. He studied at the Moscow Conservatory, and made his debut in 1926. He was a leading tenor at the Bolshoi Theatre for many years, and was particularly beloved by audiences for his beautiful soft-toned singing and his great musicality in Russian romance. He was famous for his role as Lensky in Tchaikovsky's Eugene Onegin, and his recitals of Russian romances were legendary throughout Russia.

This live recording from 1948 captures him at the height of his powers in a recital of favorite Russian romances, preserved in the Russian State Archive of Phonorecords and released here for the first time.
