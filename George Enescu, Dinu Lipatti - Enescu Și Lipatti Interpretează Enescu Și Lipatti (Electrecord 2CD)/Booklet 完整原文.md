## GEORGE ENESCU (1881-1955)

**Orchestral and Chamber Works**

Enescu studied at the Vienna and Paris Conservatories and became one of the most complete musicians of his time. The 'Jupiter Master' of the French musical school, the Romanian composer, violinist and pianist left an extraordinary legacy: four symphonies (one of them unfinished), the orchestral suite 'Vox Maris', the symphonic poem 'Symphony of the Sea', the operas 'Oedipe' and 'Œdipe', chamber music, and numerous piano pieces.

Enescu's compositions express the spirit of the Romanian people, their joy, their suffering, their customs. The music of Enescu is deeply rooted in Romanian folklore, yet it transcends national boundaries to become universal art. His works for piano and violin are particularly notable for their technical difficulty and profound musical expression.

The works presented in this recording showcase Enescu as both composer and performer, demonstrating his mastery of orchestral writing and his exceptional skills as a violinist and pianist.

## DINU LIPATTI (1917-1950)

**Piano and Chamber Works**

Dinu Lipatti was a Romanian classical pianist and composer whose musical career was cut short by his death from Hodgkin's disease at age 33. He was a pupil of Alfred Cortot and made numerous recordings for EMI.

His compositions, though few in number, are highly regarded for their neoclassical style and Romanian folk influences. The Concertino in Classical Style for piano and chamber orchestra (1936) is one of his most important works, showing the influence of Bach and Mozart while maintaining a distinctly Romanian character.
