### SCRIABIN'S SYMPHONIC PRODUCTION

Scriabin applied himself not only as a composer but for the first time in 1896, during a stay in Lyne, where he began to compose an *Allegro Symphonique*. This work was never published, and today the manuscript score is lost, and it and decided to take it up again: in thew was used with good results in the First Symphony No. 1 and *Le Divin Poème*, composed in 1905.

Between 1898 and 1900 Scriabin composed *Reverie*, op. 24, and in the Spring of the following year, *Le Divin Poème*, op. 26. In December of the same year, the composer conceived the first version of his Third Symphony, which he completed in the autumn of 1899. The work was revised several times and was finally published in 1902. The first performance was given in St. Petersburg in 1902, conducted by Anatoly Lyadov. The work was well received by the public and the critics, and Scriabin was awarded the Glinka Prize. The Third Symphony is one of the most important works of Scriabin's early period, and it shows the influence of Wagner, Liszt, and Richard Strauss. The work is in three movements: *Lutte* (Struggle), *Voluptés* (Voluptuousness), and *Jeu divin* (Divine Game). The first movement is in sonata form, with a slow introduction and a fast allegro. The second movement is a slow, lyrical movement, and the third movement is a fast, brilliant finale. The work is scored for a large orchestra, including three flutes, three oboes, three clarinets, three bassoons, four horns, three trumpets, three trombones, tuba, timpani, and strings.

### VITTORIA CARACCIOLO

Born in Reggio Calabria, graduated in piano with full marks at the Conservatory "Luigi Cherubini" in Florence under the guidance of Professor Pier Paolo Nardi. She has won several national and international prizes: 1st prize at the "Città di Senigallia" International Piano Competition, 1st prize at the "Jeunes Solistes" International Competition in Paris, 3rd prize at the "Maria Canals" International Competition in Barcelona, 1st prize at the "Luigi Cherubini" International Competition in Florence, 1st prize at the "Alexander" International Competition in Rome, and 1st prize at the "Buongiovanni" International Competition in Ancona. She has performed in recital in Italy and abroad, and as a soloist with orchestras such as the "Pomeriggi Musicali" Orchestra in Milan, the Italian Chamber Orchestra, the Halle Orchestra, and others. She has worked with conductors such as Giuseppe Pizano, Alberto Manzi, Emmanuel Krivine, and Donato Renzetti. She has performed in Hamburg, Barcelona, Paris, Tokyo, New York, London, and has recorded for BBC Radio 3 and Rai Radio3. She is currently a piano professor at the "Luigi Cherubini" Conservatory in Florence and at the "Francesco Cilea" Conservatory in Reggio Calabria.

### NUNZIO DELLO IACOVO

Born in Gioiosa Ionica (RC), graduated in 1988 in Piano at the "Giacomo Lettimi" Conservatory in Rimini with top marks, under the guidance of Piero Masci. He later attended the "Incontri col Maestro" Advanced Piano Academy in Imola, directed by Franco Scala, and in Vienna he studied with Paul Badura-Skoda. He obtained the "Diploma di Alta Perfezionamento" at the Salzburg Mozarteum and the "Diploma di Perfezionamento in Musica d'Insieme" at the "Giuseppe Verdi" Conservatory in Milan. He has won prizes in national and international piano competitions, including the "Città di Senigallia" International Piano Competition, the "Jeunes Solistes" International Competition in Paris, the "Maria Canals" International Competition in Barcelona, and others. He has performed in recital in Italy and abroad, and as a soloist with orchestras such as the "Pomeriggi Musicali" Orchestra in Milan, the Italian Chamber Orchestra, the Halle Orchestra, and others. He has worked with conductors such as Giuseppe Pizano, Alberto Manzi, Emmanuel Krivine, and Donato Renzetti. He has performed in Hamburg, Barcelona, Paris, Tokyo, New York, London, and has recorded for BBC Radio 3 and Rai Radio3. He is currently a piano professor at the "Luigi Cherubini" Conservatory in Florence and at the "Francesco Cilea" Conservatory in Reggio Calabria.
