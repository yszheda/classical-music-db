## About this Recording

This is the live recording of Puccini's *La Fanciulla del West* from 28 June 1961 in Rome, with Arturo Basile conducting the RAI Rome Symphony Orchestra and Chorus, Renata Tebaldi as Minnie, Daniele Barioni as Dick Johnson, Gian Giacomo Guelfi as Jack Rance. *La Fanciulla del West* was premiered in 1910 at the Metropolitan Opera New York, one of Puccini's most popular operas, set in the California gold rush, telling the story of the saloon girl Minnie who saves the outlaw Dick Johnson from the sheriff Jack Rance.

This 1961 live recording from Rome RAI was remastered in 24-bit/96kHz by Walhall Eternity Series and released in 2011, preserving Tebaldi's legendary assumption of the role of Minnie with Basile's idiomatic Puccini conducting.
