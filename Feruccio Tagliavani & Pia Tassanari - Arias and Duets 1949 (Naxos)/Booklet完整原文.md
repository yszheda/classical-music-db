Producer's Note

This broadcast was the American début of the excerpts from Orefice's obscure four act opera, Chopin (Teatro Lirico, Milan, 25th November 1901). These excerpts were undoubtedly inspired by Tassinari's and Tagliavini's performances of the opera at the Teatro Municipal, Rio de Janeiro, on 13th and 15th September 1949. Pianist Karl

is not credited, but was probably played by Murray Graitzer, first flautist of the San Francisco Opera Orchestra from 1948 to 1957. Note also that Tagliavini sings the Le Cid aria down a half step from score pitch.
