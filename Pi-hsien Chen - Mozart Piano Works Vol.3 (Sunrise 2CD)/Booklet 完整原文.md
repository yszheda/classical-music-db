## Liner Notes

In a letter to his father on 27 August 1778 from St. Germain in Paris, Mozart wrote: "Mr. [Johann Christian] Bach from London has been here for the past two weeks. He is going to write a French opera and has only come to hear the singers... You can only imagine his delight and mine at meeting again; perhaps his delight may not have been quite like mine at this reunion... I love him (as you know) and respect him with all my heart. As for him, there is no doubt, but that he has praised me warmly, not only face, but when others as well (and in all seriousness)."

Mozart must have opened his heart at the time: Variations on "Lison dormait" KV 264, based on an aria from Nicolas Dezède's opera comique Julie (1772) which Mozart heard performed in Paris in 1778. Still in Paris that year, Mozart had engraved, under his supervision, the six Variations in G major KV 180 which had been written in Augsburg in May, a year in which he also composed eight string quartets, various divertimentos, and several symphonies!

The eight variations in Eb major on "La Belle Françoise" KV 353 were composed in 1781-82. The Sonata KV 570 and KV 576 were written in February and July of 1789, just after Mozart's trip to Berlin. They are not exactly the "easy" sonatas of the Prince Fürstenberg, as has been assumed.

Mozart wrote to his father on 19 April 1782: "... I go every Sunday at twelve o'clock to Baron von Swieten, where nothing is played but Handel and Bach. At the moment, I'm collecting the fugues of Bach - not only those by Johann Sebastian, but also those by Emanuel [C.P.E. Bach] and Friedemann [W.F. Bach]." We often find contrapuntally developed, light, and open texture in the Mozart sonatas from this period.

It is interesting to see how - almost at the end of his life, with no established design to follow - he was introducing material of a rhetorical kind into his music and attempting to include more contrapuntal complexity, as can be seen in the Sonata in D major KV 576.

## Pi-hsien Chen (Biography)

Pi-hsien Chen was born in Taiwan and came to Cologne when she was nine years old. One year later, she was admitted in the class of Hans-Otto Schmidt-Neuhaus. At the age of 21, she won the first prize at the ARD-International Piano Competition in Munich. Later on the first prizes at the A. Schoenberg-Competition in Rotterdam and the J.S.Bach-Competition in Washington D.C.

She performed with important orchestras, such as the London Symphony Orchestra, the BBC Symphony Orchestra, the Concertgebouw Orchestra, the Zurich-Tonhalle-Orchestra, and all German Radio Symphony Orchestras. Conductors with whom she has worked, were: Bernhard Haitink, Paul Sacher, Sir Colin Davis, Charles Dutoit, Marek Janowski, Hans Zender and Peter Eötvös. She was a partner of Hermann Baumann, Pierre-Laurent Aimard, Anner Bylsma and Augustin Dumay.

Pi-hsien Chen took part in numerous music festivals: the Salzburg Festival, the Hong-Kong-Art-Festival, the Festival d'Automne Paris, Musica Strasbourg, the Tzimiside Cologne, in the German Pavilion of the EXPO 2000 in Hanover, the L'Ardente Fete Festival in France and at the Royaumont abbey near Paris; the Klavier-Festival Ruhr, the Klavierfestival Ruhr, and engagement for contemporary music grew in the co-operation with composers as Pierre Boulez, Karlheinz Stockhausen and Gyorgy Kurtag, John Cage and Elliott Carter. Since 1983, Pi-hsien Chen was a professor for the piano at the Musikhochschule, Köln and continues teaching at the Musikhochschule Freiburg since 2004.

Regularly she is giving international master classes like in Boston, (US) Walnut Hill or Helsinki, Finland (Sibelius Academy).

## 樂曲解說

1782 年 8 月 27 日，莫札特在從巴黎聖日耳曼寫給父親的信中提到：「約翰·克里斯蒂安·巴赫從倫敦來到這裡已經兩個星期了。他將寫一部法國歌劇，他只是來聽聽歌手們……你可以想像他和我再次見面的喜悅，也許他的喜悅不像我那麼強烈……你知道，我全心全意地愛他並尊敬他。至於他，毫無疑問，他熱情地讚美我，不僅當面，而且在別人面前也是如此（而且是認真的）。」

莫札特一定在那時敞開了心扉：《Lison dormait》變奏曲 KV 264，基於尼古拉·德澤德的歌劇《朱莉》(1772) 中的一首詠嘆調，莫札特於 1778 年在巴黎觀看了這部歌劇。同年，莫札特在巴黎出版了 G 大調六段變奏曲 KV 180，這部作品於 5 月在奧格斯堡創作，同年他還創作了八首弦樂四重奏、多首嬉遊曲和幾首交響曲！

降 E 大調「美麗的法國女人」八段變奏曲 KV 353 創作於 1781-82 年。奏鳴曲 KV 570 和 KV 576 分別創作於 1789 年 2 月和 7 月，就在莫札特訪問柏林之後。它們並不完全是人們普遍認為的菲斯特enberg 親王的「簡單」奏鳴曲。

莫札特於 1782 年 4 月 19 日寫信給他的父親：「……我每個星期日十二點去斯維滕男爵那裡，那裡只演奏韓德爾和巴赫的作品。目前，我正在收集巴赫的賦格曲——不僅是約翰·塞巴斯蒂安·巴赫的，還有埃馬努埃爾 [C.P.E.巴赫] 和弗里德曼 [W.F.巴赫] 的。」我們經常在莫札特這段時期的奏鳴曲中發現對位發展、輕盈和開放的結構。

有趣的是，在他生命的盡頭，沒有既定的設計可以遵循——他將修辭風格的素材引入他的音樂中，並嘗試納入更多的對位複雜性，這在 D 大調奏鳴曲 KV 576 中可以見證。
