# Johann Sebastian Bach
## St Matthew Passion BWV 244 (1727)
### (with Cantata BWV 67 "Halt im Gedächtnis Jesum Christ")

This recording of the St Matthew Passion comes from a performance given in London in 1949, with the great English contralto Kathleen Ferrier singing the alto solos, and the celebrated English tenor Peter Pears as the Evangelist. It captures a very distinguished English performance of Bach's great masterpiece from the middle of the twentieth century, one that preserves Kathleen Ferrier's legendary singing of the great alto aria *Erbarme dich*, which she made so very much her own.

Kathleen Ferrier (1912-1953) was one of the most beloved English singers of the twentieth century, whose career was cut tragically short by cancer when she was only 41. She was particularly celebrated for her performances of Bach and the English song repertoire, and her *Erbarme dich* has remained legendary ever since. This recording preserves that legendary performance from 1949, taken from the original BBC broadcast tapes, and issued here for the first time on CD in fully remastered form.

The performance is conducted by Hugo Rignold (1905-1976), a very distinguished conductor of his generation who worked extensively with the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra and broadcast many concerts with the BBC. The Glyndebourne Festival Chorus and the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra give their all under his direction, and the result is a performance that has all the discipline and the spiritual depth that the work requires.

## 第二页 (scans/HIPPO_20220418_0002.jpg)

The album also includes a performance of Bach's Cantata BWV 67 *Halt im Gedächtnis Jesum Christ*, recorded in 1947 with Reginald Jacques conducting, and with Kathleen Ferrier again singing the alto solo. This is also taken from the original BBC broadcast tapes, and it gives us another opportunity to hear Ferrier singing Bach in her prime.

Bach's St Matthew Passion is one of the supreme masterpieces of all music, setting the story of the Passion and death of Jesus Christ according to the Gospel of St Matthew, with the narrative interspersed with choruses and arias that meditate on the meaning of the events. It was first performed on Good Friday 1727 in Leipzig, and after Bach's death it fell into neglect before being revived by Mendelssohn in 1829, an event that began the great nineteenth-century Bach revival. It has been one of the most beloved of Bach's works ever since.

This recording is an important document of a historic performance with one of the greatest singers of the twentieth century in her prime. It has been fully remastered from the original BBC tapes for this release, and brings to life once again a very distinguished performance that deserves to be better known.

## 第五页 (scans/HIPPO_20220418_0005.jpg)

## The Artists

**Kathleen Ferrier** (1912-1953)

The great English contralto, one of the most beloved singers of the twentieth century. Her voice was famous for its rich, warm, expressive quality, and she moved audiences all over the world with her sincerity and directness. She was particularly celebrated for her Bach singing, and her *Erbarme dich* from the St Matthew Passion was her signature aria. This recording preserves her legendary interpretation from 1949, only four years before her early death.

**Peter Pears** (1910-1986)

The celebrated English tenor, closely associated with Benjamin Britten's music, but also a distinguished Evangelist in Bach's Passions. his clear diction and intelligent musicality made him one of the most admired Evangelists of his generation.

**Hugo Rignold** (1905-1976)

English conductor, active in London with the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra and the BBC, he conducted many important broadcasts of choral works during the middle of the twentieth century.

**Glyndebourne Festival Chorus**

One of the leading British choruses of the period, famous for their precision and musicality.

**Royal Philharmonic Orchestra**

Britain's leading symphony orchestra, at the time of this recording it was one of the finest orchestras in Europe.

## 第六页 (scans/HIPPO_20220418_0006.jpg)

**Reginald Jacques** (1894-1969)

English conductor and editor, celebrated for his editions of choral music and his conducting of Bach choral works. He conducted the Bach Cantata BWV 67 included here in this 1947 BBC broadcast.

**Elsie Suddaby** (1907-1988)

English soprano, distinguished Bach singer who performs the soprano solos on this recording.

**Eric Greene** (1912-1995)

English tenor, sings the tenor arias in the St Matthew Passion.

**Keith Falkner** (1900-1994)

English bass, distinguished singer who performs the bass arias.
