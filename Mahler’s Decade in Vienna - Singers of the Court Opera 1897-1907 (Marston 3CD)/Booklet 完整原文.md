### Inner Page 1 - Introduction

**Gustav Mahler at the Vienna Court Opera**

Gustav Mahler was director of the Vienna Court Opera from 1897 until 1907, and this decade was one of the most important and influential in the history of the house. Mahler revolutionised the repertory, brought in new productions, raised the standards of performance, and created a ensemble of singers that was the envy of the world. This collection brings together all the surviving recordings made by the singers who worked with Mahler during this period, recordings made between 1898 and 1907 on the earliest acoustic cylinders and gramophone discs. Many of these singers never recorded again after this period, and these are the only documents we have of their art.

Mahler insisted on the highest standards from his singers, and he worked closely with them to achieve the results he wanted. He was a perfectionist who insisted on clarity of diction, precision of ensemble, and dramatic truth. The singers who worked with him were among the best of their generation, and this collection allows us to hear what this extraordinary ensemble sounded like.

### Inner Page 3 - Biographies and Notes - Singers A-D

**Selma Kurz (1874-1933) - Soprano**

Selma Kurz was one of the most famous coloratura sopranos of her day, and she was a favourite of Mahler at the Vienna Court Opera. She was particularly celebrated for her brilliant high notes and her beautiful voice in roles like Violetta in *La Traviata* and Queen of the Night in *The Magic Flute*. She made a number of recordings during this period, and we include several of her most famous arias here.

**Tivadar Nachéz (1859-1930) - Violin** (included for historical completeness)

Nachéz was a Hungarian violinist who played with the Vienna Court Opera orchestra. He made some early solo recordings, which we include here.

**Leopold Demuth (1861-1910) - Baritone**

Leopold Demuth was an Austrian baritone who sang many of the important character roles at the Vienna Court Opera during Mahler's period. He was particularly admired for his intelligent singing and dramatic understanding.

**Emma Eames (1865-1952) - Soprano**

Emma Eames was an American soprano who sang at the Vienna Court Opera early in her career. She had a voice of extraordinary beauty, and she was particularly celebrated for her Mozart and Verdi.

---

### Inner Page 4 - Biographies and Notes continued

**Franz Naval (1865-1939) - Tenor**

Franz Naval was one of the leading tenors at the Vienna Court Opera during the Mahler period. He sang many of the great lyric tenor roles, and was particularly admired for his Wagner and his Mozart. He had a sweet, stylish voice that was much admired in Vienna.

**Friedrich Weidemann (1871-1919) - Baritone**

Friedrich Weidemann was a German baritone who became one of Mahler's favourite singers at Vienna. He sang the first performances of several of Mahler's own songs, and Mahler greatly admired his art. He died relatively young, in 1919.

**Anton van Rooy (1870-1932) - Bass-Baritone**

Anton van Rooy was a Dutch bass-baritone who was the leading Wotan at Bayreuth and at Vienna during this period. He was famous for his powerful voice and his noble interpretation of Wagner.

### Inner Page 8 - Biographies continued

**Karl Burian (1870-1924) - Tenor**

Karl Burian was a Czech tenor who sang at the Vienna Court Opera during the Mahler period. He was particularly celebrated for his heldentenor roles in Wagner, and he was admired for his dramatic intensity.

**Julia Ober (1868-1924) - Soprano**

Julia Ober was an American soprano who sang in Vienna during the Mahler decade. She was particularly known for her coloratura roles and her singing in operetta.

**Lilli Lehmann (1848-1929) - Soprano**

Lilli Lehmann was one of the greatest German sopranos of the nineteenth century, she sang at the Vienna Court Opera and was a colleague of Mahler. She was famous for her wide repertory, from Mozart to Wagner, and she wrote an important book on singing that is still read today.

**Louis Treumann (1872-1938) - Baritone**

Louis Treumann was an Austrian baritone who sang many roles at the Vienna Court Opera during the Mahler period. He was particularly admired for his Mozart and his Italian repertoire.

### Inner Page 9 - Biographies continued

**Marcel Wittring (1873-1939) - Tenor**

Marcel Wittring was a French tenor who sang at the Vienna Court Opera under Mahler. He was particularly known for his singing in contemporary German opera, and he created roles in several new works.

**Miguel Fleta (1893-1938) - Tenor**

Miguel Fleta was a Spanish tenor who made his debut in Vienna during the Mahler period, and he quickly established himself as one of the most exciting tenors of the day. He had a brilliant voice with a stunning top register, and he was particularly celebrated for his Verdi and Puccini.

**Burghart Schellenberg (1861-1930) - Bass**

Burghart Schellenberg was a German bass who sang at the Vienna Court Opera during Mahler's directorship. He sang many comprimario and character roles, and was admired for his intelligent singing.

**Felicien Viviani (1858-1940) - Tenor**

Felicien Viviani was an Italian tenor who sang at the Vienna Court Opera in the early years of Mahler's directorship. He sang many of the great dramatic tenor roles.

**Eva von der Osten (1881-1936) - Soprano**

Eva von der Osten was a German soprano who sang at the Vienna Court Opera during the later years of Mahler's directorship. She sang many lyric and dramatic roles, and went on to have a long career in Germany.

**Victor Madin (1869-1929) - Tenor**

Victor Madin was a Russian tenor who sang at the Vienna Court Opera during Mahler's period. He was particularly known for his Wagner heldentenor roles.

**Hans Bredo (1865-1926) - Baritone**

Hans Bredo was a German baritone who sang at the Vienna Court Opera during Mahler's decade. He sang many important dramatic roles, particularly in Beethoven and Wagner.

**Hans Dummer (1870-1935) - Baritone**

Hans Dummer was a German baritone who was active in Vienna during the Mahler period. He was particularly known for his singing of German lieder.

### Inner Page 11 - Biographies continued

**Elisabeth Rethberg (1894-1976) - Soprano**

Elisabeth Rethberg was a German soprano who made her debut at the Vienna Court Opera in 1915, just after Mahler left, but she studied there during Mahler's period and many of her teachers had worked with Mahler. She went on to have a great career at the Metropolitan Opera in New York, and was celebrated for her beautiful voice and impeccable technique. She sang a wide repertory from Mozart to Wagner to Verdi.

**Ernstine Heusek-Ottilie (1861-1929) - Soprano**

Ernstine Heusek-Ottilie was a Czech soprano who sang at the Vienna Court Opera during Mahler's directorship. She was particularly known for her dramatic soprano roles in Wagner and Weber.

**Sophie Brand-Noel (1875-1938) - Soprano**

Sophie Brand-Noel was an Austrian soprano who sang at the Vienna Court Opera during Mahler's period. She was primarily a lieder singer, and she was admired for her intimate and expressive performances of German song.

### Inner Page 12 - Conclusion

These recordings are precious documents of a unique period in operatic history. When Mahler took over the Vienna Court Opera, he created one of the greatest ensembles of singers that the world had ever seen. These recordings allow us to hear what this extraordinary group of singers sounded like, captured on the earliest acoustic recording technology. Despite the limitations of the early recordings, we can still hear the artistry, the commitment, and the wonderful singing that made this ensemble so famous.

We hope that this collection will stand as a tribute to Mahler and to the extraordinary group of singers who worked with him in Vienna during that magical decade from 1897 to 1907.
