## Page 3 (scans/HIPPO_20241218_0003.jpg) - Biography of Lukas Geniušas

### Lukas Geniušas

Lukas Geniušas was born in 1991 in Moscow into a family of musicians. He studied with Pavel Nersessian at the Moscow State Conservatory. He won second prize in the 16th International Chopin Piano Competition in Warsaw in 2010, where this recording was made. He has also won prizes in several other international competitions including the Gina Bachauer International Piano Competition and the Queen Elisabeth Competition.

His playing combines brilliant technique with deep poetic feeling and natural sensitivity to Chopin's musical language. This album contains his complete competition program from the 2010 Chopin Competition, including the monumental Third Piano Sonata, the complete 24 Préludes, the Andante spianato and Grande Polonaise brillante, and his encores. This is his debut recording release from the National Fryderyk Chopin Institute, capturing one of the most outstanding young pianists of his generation at a historic moment.

## Page 5 (scans/HIPPO_20241218_0005.jpg) - Information about the Chopin Competition

### The International Chopin Piano Competition

The International Chopin Piano Competition has been held in Warsaw since 1927. It is the most important and prestigious competition in the world for pianists who specialize in the music of Frédéric Chopin, and it has launched the careers of many of the greatest pianists of the past century. The 16th competition was held in 2010, the bicentenary of Chopin's birth, and Lukas Geniušas won second prize with his deeply communicative and technically brilliant performances that impressed both audiences and the jury.

This series of releases from the National Fryderyk Chopin Institute presents the prize-winning recitals from the competition, documenting the finest young Chopin pianists of our time for listeners around the world.
