## Renato Rossellini (19.. - 19..)

Renato Rossellini was an Italian composer of the 20th century. *La Guerra* (The War) is one of his operas dealing with the social and human effects of war, written in a lyrical-verismo style. This 1960 live recording preserves the opera with Magda Olivero in the leading role, one of the great Italian sopranos of the 20th century.

---

## Synopsis

*La Guerra* (The War) is an opera dealing with the impact of war on ordinary people and families. The story takes place in Italy during wartime, focusing on a family torn apart by conflict:

- **Act I:** Opens with radio announcement setting the wartime scene. Marta, the mother, awaits news with her daughter Maria. Her daughter Maria is in love with Erik, a young man who must go to war.
- The opera explores themes of love, loss, fear, and hope during wartime, culminating in tragic consequences for the family. The title *La Guerra* emphasizes the destructive nature of war on ordinary human lives.

## Principal Artists

### Magda Olivero (Marta)

Magda Olivero (1910-2014) was one of the most celebrated Italian sopranos of the 20th century, particularly renowned for her performances in verismo operas, where her dramatic intensity and beautiful tone were unsurpassed. She was particularly associated with roles like this one, depicting strong emotional conflict.

### Nino Sanzogno (Conductor)

Nino Sanzogno (1918-2005) was an Italian conductor, known for his work in Italian opera and especially for his performances of contemporary Italian operas. He conducted this 1960 performance for Italian radio.
