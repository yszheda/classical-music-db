## Synopsis

### Act One
Disguised as a paladin and masquerading under the assumed name of her brother Ricciardo, Bradamante enters in search of her fiancé, Ruggiero. In the company of her tutor, Melisso, who is similarly disguised as a knight, she has landed on an island ruled by the sorceress Alcina. Together they penetrate to the island's interior, a wilderness surrounded by mountains. Here they encounter Alcina's sister, Morgana, who immediately falls in love with "Ricciardo". The mountain bursts open, revealing Alcina's magnificent palace. The sorceress herself enters on the arm of Ruggiero, whom she genuinely loves and who is entirely in thrall to her charms. She welcomes Bradamante and Melisso as her guests. Bradamante tries to confront Ruggiero, but Ruggiero has completely forgotten his fiancée.

### Act Two
While Ruggiero, still in the palace, expresses his yearning for Alcina, Melissō appears to him in the form of his former tutor, Atlante. He upbraids Ruggiero for betraying his ideals and gives him a ring that exposes the blandishments of Alcina's island as an enchantment. Ruggiero is convinced that he has been enchanted, he loves Alcina and secretly prepares to escape. Bradamante once again reveals her true identity, but in spite of the fact that his love for her has been rekindled, Ruggiero rejects Alcina because he is afraid that she will again try to trick him. Alcina will again try to capture him.

Close to the palace gardens, Alcina is in the process of turning Ricciardo into a wild animal in order to dispel Ruggiero's suspicions. Morgana stops her, and Ruggiero, who now comes running in, declares that Alcina's intentions are sufficient to calm him down. He says that he forgives Riccardo. Morgana claims that Ricciardo loves not Alcina but herself, and Ruggiero receives permission to arm himself and go hunting to clear his mind. Oronte enters and tells Alcina that Ruggiero is in despair but he has finally become reconciled with Bradamante, who is in fact a woman. Morgana is recognized by Ruggiero as the true identity. Ruggiero prophesies the imminent end of Alcina's magic realm. In a subterranean vault, Alcina calls on demons to help her, but she is soon forced to realize that her magic wand has lost its power.

### Act Three
In the entrance hall of Alcina's palace, Morgana, disappointed in love, tries to rekindle her relationship with her former lover, Oronte. Although Oronte still loves her, he pretends for the present to be indifferent towards her. Alcina and Ruggiero meet by chance, provoking a confrontation between them. He admits that he wants to leave her, attributing his decision to martial prowess and his love for Bradamante. Alcina's anguish and her memory of their happiness together leave him unmoved. Melisso reports that the island is surrounded by warriors and wild animals. Ruggiero is determined to fight. Oronte tells Alcina that her armies have been defeated and that the whole island is threatened. In despair she recognizes that her magic cannot save her, and after a final struggle with Bradamante and Ruggiero, Alcina once more deploys all her arts of enchantment against Ruggiero. But in vain: neither she nor Morgana can prevent Ruggiero from using Alcina's magic ring to shatter the urn that holds the magic. The palace and its surroundings crumble, and the enchanted beings are restored to human form. They celebrate the happy outcome and an end to their sufferings.

Bien qu'Oronte l'aime encore, il feint pour le moment d'être indifférent à elle. Alcina et Ruggiero se rencontrent par hasard, et une dispute éclate entre eux. Il admet qu'il veut la quitter, attribue sa décision à la force des armes et son amour pour Bradamante. L'angoisse d'Alcina et le souvenir du bonheur qu'ils ont eu ensemble ne l'émouvant pas plus. Melisso rapporte que l'île est entourée de guerriers et d'animaux sauvages. Ruggiero est déterminé à se battre. Oronte dit à Alcina que ses armées sont battues et que toute l'île est menacée. En désespoir de cause elle appelle les démons à l'aide, mais elle doit vite comprendre que sa baguette magique a perdu son pouvoir.

Plus de pouvoir, il ne reste plus à Alcina que de l'accepter. Son enchantement est terminé, les êtres enchantés redeviennent hommes, et ils célèbrent la fin de leurs souffrances et l'heureux dénouement.

Translation: Reinhard Lüthje
